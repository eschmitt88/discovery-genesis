## Reasoning
The abstract foregrounds a technique combination ("using a combination of quantitative 13C and 2D HSQC NMR techniques") as the enabling step, and reports its findings in terms of what was clearly resolvable versus ambiguous — a signature of means-first genesis, where the measurement instrument determines what can be said, rather than a stated hypothesis about LCC linkage abundance driving the design. Both component NMR techniques (quantitative 13C, 1985; quantitative 2D HSQC, 2003) already existed; the paper's contribution is applying them together to lignin fractions isolated with an established procedure (Björkman, 1956). Evidence that would change my mind: if the abstract had stated a specific prior expectation about LCC linkage abundance that the NMR data then confirmed or refuted, which would push toward idea-first.

## Alternative reading
Accretion is a live alternative, and notably this paper closely parallels its topic-year-matched pair (bark/wood lignin characterization) in structure: one more species/fraction characterization study in a long-running lignin-chemistry NMR literature using a fairly standard toolkit. I lean means-first over accretion because the abstract explicitly foregrounds the technique combination as newly enabling a previously "ambiguous" quantification (LCC linkages specifically), suggesting a genuine methodological step rather than pure repetition on new material.
