## Reasoning
The paper's own sequence of events is screen-first: a triple mutant (ΔpvdA ΔpchE ΔfeoB) still grows in iron-chelated media, so the authors ran unbiased Tn5 mutagenesis and picked the single strongest hit (PA2374/TseF). Everything downstream — its proximity to H3-T6SS (2009 ref), its MORN motifs (2006 ref, borrowed from eukaryotic cell biology), its interaction with PQS (2006 ref) and OMVs (2005/2010 refs) — is explanatory scaffolding built around a screening hit, not a design that preceded the experiment. That pattern (means reveal idea) is the definitional case for means-first. Evidence that would change my mind: an introduction sentence stating they specifically hypothesized T6SS-mediated iron delivery before screening (would push toward problem-first/idea-first).

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is plausible: iron acquisition completeness is an old, well-studied problem, and T6SS effector biology (established ~2010-2013) was a recently mature framework they could apply to interrogate genes near secretion loci. Less likely because the actual mechanism (PQS+OMV cargo, not direct toxin delivery) was not predictable from prior T6SS-effector work and had to be discovered empirically after the screen hit.
