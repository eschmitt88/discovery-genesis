## Reasoning
This paper's genre is genuinely mixed — a mechanistic review with a small embedded original clinical observation (42 patients, ambulatory blood pressure monitoring) — but the abstract, title, and overall structure foreground synthesis over finding: the abstract never mentions the original data at all, and the embedded observation functions as an illustration within a larger argument rather than as the paper's stated contribution. Per codebook §1's accretion test, the conclusion's form (amyloid clearance, not just production, matters; several converging risk factors impair it) could have been outlined before the 42-patient dataset was analyzed, with only that one paragraph's numbers left blank.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is plausible if the 42-patient nocturnal-blood-pressure-dipping analysis is read as the paper's real (if minor) empirical contribution: a specific, named hypothesis (does blunted nocturnal BP dipping associate with impaired amyloid clearance in AD) tested with existing ambulatory monitoring technology as the pre-existing ingredient. I rate accretion higher because that analysis is not signposted anywhere in the abstract or title, and reads as one supporting illustration among several literatures being synthesized, not the organizing question of the paper.
