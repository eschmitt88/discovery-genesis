## Reasoning
The paper is explicit that its own earlier work established the intrathecal-tracer/MRI method but only within selected regions of interest, and this study's advance is scaling that unmodified method up to whole-brain, longer-timescale coverage -- a clean instance of the v1.1 "serial reuse of the authors' own established platform on a next target" tell (here, "next target" being broader anatomical/temporal scope rather than a new species or population). Full text and repeated self-citation (2013, 2015, 2017) support high confidence. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible given the explicit self-citation trail.

## Alternative reading
A cross-species-translation framing is also visible and worth flagging: the paper explicitly states its findings "translate previous findings in animal studies into humans," which resembles the animal→human translation pattern noted in v1.1 issue #5 as awkward to fit cleanly into transfer or recombination. I did not use a translation label since none exists in the current move-label vocabulary, and coded the paper's most concrete/verifiable move (extending an established human method to whole-brain scope) as gap-filling:first-measurement instead.
