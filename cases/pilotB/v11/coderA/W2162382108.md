## Reasoning
The paper's own methods section cites the methoxyl-determination method to the lead author's earlier 2003 paper -- a clean instance of the v1.1 tell for problem-first (an established method, reused from the authors' own prior work, applied to a next target material). All other analytical techniques used are decades-old, well-established methods from other groups, not new capabilities introduced here. The paper explicitly frames the gap as target-specific (characteristics of paper mulberry/mulberry bark, not kenaf, which is already well studied), fitting gap-filling:next-target precisely. Full text and an unambiguous self-citation make this a high-confidence call. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible.

## Alternative reading
Accretion is a weak alternative if the paper is read purely as a survey/cataloguing exercise across three plant species with no comparative hypothesis; I judged problem-first because the paper explicitly frames and tests a specific structural question (does bark lignin share kenaf bast fiber's favorable syringyl/erythro-β-O-4 profile) rather than merely aggregating measurements.
