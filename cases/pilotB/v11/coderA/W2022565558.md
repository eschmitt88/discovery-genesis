## Reasoning
With only a title and reference list, the strongest available signal is the repeated citation of the authors' own plasma-source apparatus papers (1995, 1996, 2004) as background/method alongside very recent (2009-2011) bulk-Mg2Sn thermoelectric characterization papers. That pattern -- an established in-house instrument applied to a newly topical target material -- is exactly the v1.1 "serial reuse of one's own platform on a new target" tell that resolves the means-first/problem-first ambiguity toward problem-first. Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing the plasma-deposition approach itself required a substantive new modification to work with Mg2Sn (which would push toward means-first).

## Alternative reading
Means-first is plausible if the multi-dipolar plasma technique had to be substantially adapted (a real capability advance) to handle the specific chemistry/volatility challenges of Mg2Sn co-sputtering; this cannot be assessed without full text.
