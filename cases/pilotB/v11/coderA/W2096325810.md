## Reasoning
Both the explicit "has not been observed... we present the first" language and the repeated self-citation of the group's own earlier waveguide-array papers (used for a different phenomenon, Bloch oscillations/dynamic localization) as method background satisfy the v1.1 problem-first tell cleanly: an established platform, not modified in any fundamental way here, applied to close a specific, recently-opened, named gap. Full text makes this a high-confidence call. Evidence that would change my mind: if the paper had needed to develop a materially new fabrication or excitation capability specifically to reach the flat-band regime (e.g., a new DOE-based excitation scheme not present in prior group papers) -- but the Methods instead describe standard slit-beam-shaping fabrication and a phase-controlled DOE excitation setup built from established components.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a weak alternative: the phase-controlled four-spot excitation setup (DOE + spatial filter) is a genuinely careful piece of optical engineering built specifically for this experiment. If that excitation scheme is judged a new-to-the-field capability rather than routine application of established optics, the call could shift to means-first. I judged it as an application of established techniques (slit-beam shaping, DOE diffraction) rather than a new capability.
