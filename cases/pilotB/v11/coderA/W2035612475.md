## Reasoning
The abstract describes a stated conceptual proposition ("we suggest," "we propose") that organizes the rest of the paper's argument, matching the v1.1 rule that framework-proposing papers with no data get idea-first when the proposition organizes the design. The environmental-psychology literature (Bronfenbrenner 1979, cited with very high impact) is drawn on to unify two previously separate research traditions -- environmental/ecological psychology and evidence-based healthcare-design research -- which is why unification, not mere consolidation, is the central move. Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing the paper is primarily a literature review with the "ecological theory" mentioned only in passing (which would push toward accretion or no — essay).

## Alternative reading
Given no full text, this could instead be an essay/perspective piece (is_primary: no) rather than a genuine framework paper, if the "ecological theory" is asserted rhetorically without any structural elaboration. The abstract alone cannot distinguish a testable/applicable structure from rhetorical framing, which is exactly why the codebook caps confidence here.
