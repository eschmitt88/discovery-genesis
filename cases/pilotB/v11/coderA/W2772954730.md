## Reasoning
The paper explicitly claims priority for a specific, well-defined measurement gap (exergaming's effect on lipid/inflammatory/adipokine markers), and both the exercise intervention and the assay methods are standard, established techniques not modified for this study. That is a clean problem-first, gap-filling:first-measurement case with strong textual support, warranting high confidence. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible given the explicit priority claim.

## Alternative reading
Accretion is not plausible (this is a hypothesis-driven pre/post intervention study, not an aggregation). Means-first is a weak alternative only if the exergaming intervention protocol itself (heart-rate-monitored, difficulty-graded Just Dance sessions) were treated as a novel capability; I judged it as a standard, previously used exergaming protocol.
