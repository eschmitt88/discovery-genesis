## Reasoning
The paper's own methods section is unusually transparent about the theory-building process: the COM-B proposition is derived first (from two independent traditions) and then used to organize the systematic review's synthesis and the reliability testing that follows -- a textbook idea-first structure with a stated proposition preceding and shaping the design. Full text makes this a high-confidence call. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible given the explicit sequencing described.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is a weak alternative only if the systematic review of 19 existing frameworks is treated as the paper's real starting point (an inductive synthesis from which COM-B was later extracted, rather than a proposition stated first). The text's own account contradicts this reading, but the systematic-review methodology section is presented alongside the theory narrative, which could support a slightly different reconstruction of the actual research process.
