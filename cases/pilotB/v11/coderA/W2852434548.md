## Reasoning
Both named sub-fields being joined here -- Open Information Extraction and named-entity-linking/KG-construction -- sit within the same broader field (Semantic Web/NLP), so recombination (not transfer) is the correct move label per the v1.1 boundary rule. The paper's motivating gap (OpenIE underexplored specifically for KG construction, despite established use for other tasks) is explicit and specific, and the ingredients closing it are established tools combined in a new pipeline rather than a new instrument. Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing the entity-linking/grammatical-association strategies themselves required a substantively new algorithmic contribution (which would push toward means-first).

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a plausible alternative if the specific integration strategies (associating OpenIE propositions with grammatical units, selecting information elements for RDF triples) constitute a genuinely new capability rather than routine combination of existing tools; abstract-only evidence cannot fully resolve this, since the abstract emphasizes the pipeline's design decisions as the contribution.
