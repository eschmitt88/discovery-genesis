## Reasoning
The trial itself, the mutation-sequencing method, and the tumor-sample bank all pre-existed this specific analysis; the paper's contribution is a targeted secondary/exploratory question (interaction between mutation subtype and treatment duration) answered by reanalyzing existing resources with more follow-up time, not by new data collection or new instrumentation. This fits problem-first cleanly. Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing the mutation-analysis pipeline itself was substantially new/expanded relative to the original trial report (which would push toward means-first).

## Alternative reading
gap-filling:bigger-n is a plausible alternative label (same trial cohort, but with a longer follow-up window than the original report) rather than gap-filling:other; I judged the central move as answering a genuinely new question (subtype-duration interaction) rather than simply extending sample size or duration of the same original question, but the two labels are close here.
