## Reasoning
Per the v1.1 rule for reviews with an embedded, unsignposted dataset, I followed the abstract's own framing (a literature review/perspective piece) for `is_primary` and `genesis_model`, while noting the embedded 42-patient ambulatory-blood-pressure dataset explicitly in `contribution` rather than treating it as the paper's central move. The review's overall shape -- synthesizing an established mechanistic discovery (glymphatic system, 2012) with established risk-factor literature (APOE, sleep, circadian blood pressure) into an integrated pathogenesis narrative -- fits accretion/consolidation cleanly. Evidence that would change my mind: if the 42-patient dataset were foregrounded in the title/abstract as the paper's primary finding (it is not), which would instead argue for problem-first with the dataset as the closing ingredient.
