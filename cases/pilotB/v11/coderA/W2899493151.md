## Reasoning
The real-time PCR approach for species-level fruit-fly identification is a well-established field-level methodology, repeatedly applied by (largely different) groups to successive target species over more than a decade; this paper's contribution is designing and validating new primer/probe sets for two specific species not previously covered, which fits gap-filling:next-target cleanly. The specific assay is new-here (enabler: new-assay-or-method) even though the general approach is established, mirroring the same distinction used for W2030763722. Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing the assay design required a genuinely novel methodological advance beyond routine primer/probe design (which would push toward means-first).

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a weak alternative if the specific in silico specificity-validation approach used is itself treated as a meaningful new capability rather than routine primer design; abstract-only evidence favors reading this as standard application of an established assay class.
