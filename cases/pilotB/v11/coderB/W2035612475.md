## Reasoning
This is a no-data conceptual/perspective paper whose stated contribution is a proposed framework, so I applied the v1.1 rule directly: `is_primary: partial — framework`, `idea-first` because the structure (ecological theory adapted from human-development psychology) organizes the paper's argument rather than following from a narrow empirical gap. The source field for the ecological framework (developmental/environmental psychology, 1979-2009 references) differs from the target field (healthcare facility design/health policy), which makes `transfer` the most central move label — the paper imports a framework across a field boundary rather than recombining within healthcare-design literature.

## Alternative reading

I weighted idea-first higher because the abstract's own structure (AIM/BACKGROUND/CONCLUSIONS) states the proposed concept as the paper's product, not a gap-closing answer to a pre-specified question — and the v1.1 rule 6 explicitly directs framework-with-no-data papers to idea-first when the structure organizes the design.
