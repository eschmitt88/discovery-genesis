## Reasoning
This is an unusually explicit case of the v1.1 problem-first tell: the methods section states outright that the same tissue array was "used in our previous study," confirming the authors are applying their own established platform to a new candidate gene rather than introducing new capability. RIG-I itself is an established molecule (known PRR biology, already linked to colorectal cancer prognosis) being tested in a new disease context (gastric cancer) — the textbook `gap-filling:next-target` pattern (next cancer type / next candidate gene on the same tissue resource).

## Alternative reading
means-first would require the specific combination of IHC correlation analysis + RNAi knockdown + microarray profiling to be a novel methodological package, but each component is standard, off-the-shelf molecular biology methodology used identically to how it's used elsewhere in the cancer-biomarker literature — I see no capability-newness case.

## Notes for the codebook
