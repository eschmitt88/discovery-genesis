## Reasoning
Every technical ingredient — MWL/MAL isolation, quantitative 13C NMR, 2D HSQC NMR — is cited to established prior work spanning 1954-2009, much of it from the broader lignin-NMR-characterization literature (not obviously all one lab's own lineage, but a mature, shared methodological toolkit for the subfield). The paper's contribution is a specific quantitative measurement (LCC linkage-type distribution in poplar) not previously reported at this resolution, which fits `gap-filling:first-measurement` under problem-first: the method is not new, but the specific quantity measured in this specific system is.

## Alternative reading
means-first would apply if "combining" 13C and 2D HSQC NMR in this way were itself a first for the field rather than routine practice — the abstract's phrasing ("by using a combination of...") is ambiguous on this point, and several cited 2003-2006 papers already use quantitative NMR combinations for lignin, which weighs toward the combination being established rather than novel here.
