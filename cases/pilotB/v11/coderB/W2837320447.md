## Reasoning
The MRI tracer technique (intrathecal gadobutrol, confined by the intact blood-brain barrier) is established and cited to the authors' own and others' earlier, ROI-limited human studies; no new instrument or assay is introduced. What's new is scope (brain-wide rather than selected ROIs) and species-context (explicitly translating animal-model glymphatic findings into humans) — exactly the pattern the v1.1 rule names as `gap-filling:next-target` ("includes animal→human translation"). I also listed `gap-filling:first-measurement` because the brain-wide, longitudinal measurement itself (as opposed to ROI snapshots) is a specific quantity measured for the first time in this system.

## Alternative reading
means-first is a plausible alternative if the whole-brain FreeSurfer-based longitudinal quantification pipeline is treated as a new capability rather than routine application of segmentation software to a known tracer technique — but the paper doesn't claim this pipeline itself as a methodological innovation; it claims the *result* (brain-wide human evidence) as the contribution, which keeps this on the problem-first side.
