## Reasoning
No original data, cohort, or method appears anywhere in the full text — this is a narrative review published in a journal's "problem articles" (topical review) section, aggregating two literatures (the established amyloid-cascade hypothesis and the more recently characterized glymphatic-clearance system) into a synthesized account. That satisfies accretion under the codebook's test. I coded the move as `unification` despite the accretion genesis_model because the paper's explicit rhetorical move is presenting two previously largely separate bodies of work (amyloid pathology and CSF/glymphatic physiology) as reflecting one underlying mechanism — worth noting for the codebook that move labels can characterize *what a review argues* even when genesis_model is accretion, since the codebook doesn't restrict move_candidates to non-review genres.

## Alternative reading
