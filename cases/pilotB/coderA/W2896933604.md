## Reasoning
Problem-first fits: a specific, recently-raised concern (declining spinosad efficacy in one region) is the named open question, and it is answered using an existing, "widely implemented" bioassay protocol rather than a new instrument or method. This is not means-first because the bioassay long pre-dates this study and is explicitly called routine/standard; the paper's motivation is the field reports, not a new testing capability becoming available.

## Alternative reading
Accretion is a weak alternative — this could look like a routine resistance-monitoring report, but the paper makes a specific novel claim (first confirmed US occurrence, with a five-generation selection experiment showing continued tolerance increase) rather than aggregating routine surveillance numbers, so problem-first is the better fit.
