## Reasoning
The introduction is explicit that both halves of the paper — the trend description and the correlate analysis — draw on an existing survey program and an existing correlate literature ("Based on the factors mentioned in existing literature and taking account of availability in the dataset"). No new measurement instrument, no new theoretical claim, and no anomaly are proposed; the paper's contribution is bringing the trend line up to date (through the newly available 2015 wave) and testing known predictors against it. That is the accretion pattern almost by the codebook's own example ("a large trial of an established question").

## Alternative reading
Second-most-likely: problem-first, if the closing of the gender gap is read as the sharper, more specific question the paper answers using a recently-completed ingredient (the 2015 wave, without which the 20-year gender-gap-closure claim could not be made). I keep this as runner-up rather than primary because the paper's own stated aims (trend description + correlate testing) are broader and more consolidative than a single sharply-stated proposition about the gender gap.
