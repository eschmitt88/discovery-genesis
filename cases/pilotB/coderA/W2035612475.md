## Reasoning
No full text is available, but the abstract's structure alone (AIM/BACKGROUND/CONCLUSIONS, no METHODS, purely argumentative verbs like "explores," "suggests," "proposed") is a strong signal this is a perspective/conceptual essay rather than empirical or review-of-evidence work. Per decision order: not accretion (no aggregate/survey/database is reported — it's an argument, not a synthesis of external measurements); not means-first (no capability described); not problem-first in the strict sense (no specific existing ingredient is deployed to close a named gap — nothing is "closed," a new theoretical stance is proposed). That leaves idea-first: a proposition ("EBD needs an ecological theory / integrated healthscape strategies") that could have been stated before the essay was written, with the whole piece elaborating it.

## Alternative reading
Accretion is a plausible near-miss if this is read as a review-style survey of the EBD literature's shortcomings — but the paper's stated contribution is a new proposed concept/theory, not an aggregated report of the field's state, so I favored idea-first.
