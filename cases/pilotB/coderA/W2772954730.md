## Reasoning
I weighed problem-first (there is an implicit question: "does exergaming work like real exercise?") against means-first, and chose means-first because the paper's organizing device is the availability of a specific new consumer technology, not a named prior open question in the exercise-physiology literature. The move is transfer rather than recombination because the source (a commercial gaming/entertainment product) sits in a different primary field than the target (clinical exercise physiology) — a textbook transfer case per the codebook's boundary rule.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is a reasonable alternative if one treats "do known exercise benefits generalize to exergaming" as the pre-existing named question and the console as merely the ingredient that answers it — under that reading this would be problem-first with the same transfer/gap-filling move labels. I ranked means-first slightly higher given how strongly the introduction foregrounds the technology itself.
