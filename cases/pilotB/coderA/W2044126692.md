## Reasoning
Problem-first: the specific target (cervical lesions, this population) is explicitly new, while the closing ingredient (p73 genotyping paradigm, already applied to several other cancers by 2005-2011 per the reference list) pre-exists. This is not means-first because no new assay or dataset-collection capability drives the paper — PCR-RFLP is decades-old and the samples are archived from 1998-2002, years before this presumably later publication/analysis.

## Alternative reading
Could be read as accretion-adjacent "pattern extension" (one more cancer type added to a known polymorphism-cancer association literature), but the paper reports a new specific quantitative finding (OR=2.39, sharpened in high-parity subgroup) rather than aggregating existing numbers, so problem-first remains the better fit.
