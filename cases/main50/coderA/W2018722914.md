## Reasoning
REM addition to steel is cited across a dozen different steel grades in the reference list going back to the 1970s — this is squarely the "mature platform applied to next target" pattern the v1.1 rule assigns to problem-first, not means-first: the capability (REM addition + standard microscopy) is neither introduced here nor new to the field. The open question is narrow and specific to H13 (which dosage, if any, helps this particular alloy), which is what changed. Evidence that would shift this: if the introduction claimed a novel REM delivery method rather than just a new dosage series.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is weaker here — the paper does not present the characterization suite (OM/SEM/TEM) as a new capability; it is routine metallurgical practice used to explain an already-expected effect.

## Notes for the codebook
