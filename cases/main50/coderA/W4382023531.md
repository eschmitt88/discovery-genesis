## Reasoning
The polymerization chemistry and characterization techniques (cyclic voltammetry, XPS, FT-IR) are all established, applied previously to iron, copper, and magnesium by other groups per the reference list; this paper's contribution is extending the same chemistry to a new substrate metal (aluminum), directly matching gap-filling:next-target. Evidence that would change my mind: a full text showing a structurally new dithiol monomer rather than the already-known DAN compound applied to a new substrate.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is weak here since no new instrument or reagent is introduced — the "new-to-authors" tell instead points toward problem-first, matching a field-wide (not necessarily self-citation) reuse pattern of an established coating chemistry across substrate metals.
