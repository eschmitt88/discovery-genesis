## Reasoning
The audit method (patient interview plus chart review for TS use) is established and reused across many cited prior studies in different hospitals/countries; this paper's distinguishing move is applying it to a broader population (all disease types, not one condition or region), which is gap-filling:next-target. Evidence that would change my mind: a full text describing a new screening instrument rather than the standard interview-based audit.

## Alternative reading
gap-filling:bigger-n is a weaker alternative if this is read primarily as a larger sample within an identical single-hospital design already used elsewhere — I favor next-target because the abstract explicitly frames the novelty as scope (community/primary-care level, all diseases) rather than sample size alone.

## Notes for the codebook
Clinical-audit papers replicating an established survey design at a new site are common and low-ambiguity next-target cases once the reference list shows the same design repeated elsewhere.
