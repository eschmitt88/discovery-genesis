## Reasoning
The abstract is purely results-and-recommendation framed, but the reference list situates the study squarely within an existing, well-populated literature of sewage-sludge dose-response trials on pasture/grassland soils across different regions and soil types (Zimbabwe 1999, Spain 2000, various reviews 2006-2008). The DTPA extraction method used to quantify plant-available metals is over 35 years old and standard.
