## Reasoning
ddRADseq is an established method (cited 2012) applied here to generate nuclear data for a specific taxon for the first time — the paper's own framing is explicitly about supplying the nuclear-genomic test that the mtDNA-only literature lacked for this species, which is the first-measurement gap-filling pattern. Evidence that would change my mind: if these authors had already published ddRADseq data for this exact taxon previously (making this a bigger-n extension instead).

## Alternative reading
gap-filling:next-target is a close alternative (established method, new species target) — I favor first-measurement because the abstract frames the contribution around resolving a specific unmeasured discordance in this taxon, not simply extending coverage to a new species in a survey sense.

## Notes for the codebook
Cryptic-species-via-nuclear-confirmation papers are a recurring first-measurement pattern in molecular ecology; the distinguishing signal from next-target is whether the paper is testing a specific pre-existing hypothesis (first-measurement) or systematically surveying a new taxon with no prior hypothesis (next-target).
