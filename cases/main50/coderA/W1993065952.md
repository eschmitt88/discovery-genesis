## Reasoning
The abstract itself reads as an inventory of separately-established findings (immunoglobulin isotypes, pIgR structure, IEL phenotypes, enteritis histology, vaccine encapsulation approaches, microbiota effects), each traceable to distinct prior studies rather than a single unifying experiment. Per codebook §1 rule 1, the review's form (a comparative survey of teleost vs. mammalian mucosal immunology) could be sketched before assembling the specific findings. Accretion, `consolidation`.

## Alternative reading
`problem-first` is not supported per codebook §7 — no single unsatisfactory prior state is named; the abstract instead surveys the field topic by topic and flags several open questions (pIgR transcytosis, immune mechanisms behind probiotic effects) without treating any one as the paper's driving gap.

Comprehensive review that spans multiple sub-questions, several explicitly flagged as still open ("needs further investigation," "much more attention has to be paid to") — a clean accretion case, but worth noting that reviews which end by nominating specific future gaps (rather than only surveying) can tempt a problem-first misread; the codebook's existing accretion test handled this correctly here.
