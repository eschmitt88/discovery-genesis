## Reasoning
Unlike serial-platform-reuse cases, here the tracer tools themselves (fluorescent conjugate, antibody) are explicitly stated as designed and synthesized for this specific study — a capability new to the authors and, as a purpose-built carabrone-specific reagent, effectively new to the field for this compound. This is means-first: the new capability directly produces the paper's central observation and claim. Evidence that would change my mind: if these tools were shown to be reused unmodified from the authors' own prior published work.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is a weaker alternative if immunofluorescence/fluorescent-conjugate imaging in general is treated as the "ingredient" (established, decades old) rather than the specific carabrone-targeted reagents; I favor means-first because the specific reagents, not the generic imaging modality, are what the paper introduces and what drives the finding.
