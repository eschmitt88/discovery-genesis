## Reasoning
Genre and framing both point to a narrative review: the title names it as such, and the reference list spans many unrelated conditions (shoulder, lumbar, ankle, lymphedema, patellofemoral pain) in a pattern typical of a synthesis rather than a single empirical study. Per codebook §1.1 the test is whether the conclusion's form could be written with only the numbers blank — for "some benefit shown but better-quality studies needed," yes. is_primary is `no — review` per §6. genesis_confidence is capped at low because evidence is tldr-only.
