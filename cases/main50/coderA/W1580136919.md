## Reasoning
The paper names a specific, freshly opened question (is the CB1-active metabolite neurotoxic to developing neurons) that follows directly from a 2005-2008 mechanistic discovery cited in the references, and answers it with an unremarkable in vitro toxicity assay. That is problem-first with the ingredient (assay) pre-existing and ordinary, and the question being the new part — matching codebook §1.3. gap-filling:first-measurement fits because this is literally the first measurement of this specific toxicity in this specific system.

## Alternative reading
Means-first would apply if the "developing mouse cortical neuron" in vitro model itself were a new capability for these authors, but no citation marks it as new, and it reads as a standard toxicology platform.
