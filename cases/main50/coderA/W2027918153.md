## Reasoning
The reference list shows two independently mature lines converging: folded-reflector antenna geometry (1998, 2002) and this group's own liquid-crystal tunable-substrate reflectarray work, cited repeatedly from 2006 through 2013 — the v1.1 tell for problem-first (same method cited 4-8x in the authors' own prior work). Nothing in the abstract suggests a new LC formulation, fabrication process, or measurement technique; the contribution is architectural — combining folded geometry with an already-proven tuning mechanism to solve a known blockage/profile limitation. That is recombination of two established components within the same field (antenna engineering), problem-first.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is plausible if the "appropriate phase adjustment" tuning scheme on the folded upper reflector required a genuinely new LC-driving technique not present in the group's 2006-2013 work — the abstract's phrasing ("tuned with a bias voltage configuration to obtain an appropriate phase adjustment") is generic enough that this can't be ruled out without full text, which is why confidence is capped at low.
