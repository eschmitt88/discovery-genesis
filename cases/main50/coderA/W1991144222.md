## Reasoning
The full text contains a direct authorial gap statement ("to the best of our knowledge the level of BRAF expression... has not been investigated as a potential predictive and prognostic factor"), and the reference list shows the field's existing biomarker paradigm (BRAF/NRAS mutation status, established since 2002 and cited thousands of times) is mutation-based, not expression-based. The methods used to measure expression (RNA extraction, microarray/quantification) are themselves established (cited 1999-2004) — no new instrument. Problem-first, `gap-filling:first-measurement`: a quantity (expression level as opposed to mutation status) measured as a predictive/prognostic factor for the first time in this system.

## Alternative reading
`gap-filling:next-target` is a weaker alternative if expression-level biomarker analysis had already been done in other cancers and this is simply melanoma as the next target — but the explicit "to the best of our knowledge... has not been investigated" framing is specifically about the biomarker type in melanoma, not a species/cancer-type extension, so first-measurement fits better.

## Notes for the codebook
Another clean example distinguishing `first-measurement` (new *type* of quantity/variable measured, here expression level vs. mutation status) from `next-target` (same quantity, new population) — worth a codebook note contrasting the two using this card alongside the IL12RB1 and CI-electrode cards.
