## Reasoning
The GIS+AHP groundwater-potential-mapping approach is a mature, widely-replicated methodology — the reference list alone shows near-identical studies applied to river basins and regions across India, Ghana, Zimbabwe, Brazil, and Iran spanning nearly 25 years. This paper applies the same 12-layer thematic weighted-overlay approach (explicitly described using the same equation form as a cited 2010 study) to one specific basin not previously mapped this way. No new instrument, algorithm, or theory is introduced. Problem-first, `gap-filling:next-target` — established method, new target (this specific, previously unmapped basin).

## Alternative reading
`gap-filling:first-measurement` could apply if framed as "first groundwater-potential measurement for this basin" rather than "next basin for an established method" — the two readings largely coincide here, but I favored next-target since the paper's own framing ("as an example") and the reference list's dozens of directly analogous basin-specific applications point to a routine case-study extension rather than a novel measurement type.

## Notes for the codebook
A very clean, high-volume `next-target` pattern in applied environmental/earth science: "established multi-criteria GIS methodology applied to the next unstudied watershed/region" recurs constantly in this literature (dozens of near-duplicate precedents visible in a single reference list) and is one of the easiest genesis calls in the whole batch.
