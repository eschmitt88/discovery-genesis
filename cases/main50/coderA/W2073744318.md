## Reasoning
The paper names an explicit, narrow gap (lack of practical guidance resources) that is filled using already-established technology and accumulated field experience rather than a new capability or a specific empirical open question — the closest fit among the gap-filling sub-labels is `:other`, since the gap is procedural/documentation rather than next-target, bigger-n, or first-measurement. `is_primary: partial — protocol/guide` reflects that the contribution is a how-to artefact rather than a finding.

## Alternative reading
`no — review` could also fit if this reads more as a synthesis of published validation/accuracy literature than as original practical guidance, but the abstract's explicit "practical solutions to optimize image quality" framing and its likely reliance on the authors' own imaging-lab experience (not purely citation-based) pushes it toward a protocol/guide genre rather than a pure literature review.

## Notes for the codebook
Practical "how-to" guide papers (distinct from literature reviews and from dataset/tool papers) are a recognisable genre this dossier set contains; `gap-filling:other` captures the genesis reasonably, but the codebook's sub-label definitions don't explicitly anticipate "the gap is a missing guide/documentation resource" as a case — worth a note if this recurs.
