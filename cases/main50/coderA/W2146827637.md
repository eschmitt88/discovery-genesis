## Reasoning
This is a single-gene functional-characterization study within a large, well-mapped gene family (WRKY transcription factors), following a pipeline (expression profiling, phylogenetic placement, overexpression, pathogen challenge, defense-marker qPCR) that the reference list shows applied repeatedly to close relatives — Arabidopsis AtWRKY18/40/60 (2006) and other individual Populus WRKY genes (PtoWRKY23, 2009). No new instrument or assay is introduced; the "gap" is specifically that this one gene, among many characterized family members, had not yet been studied. Problem-first, `gap-filling:next-target` (established method, new target = this specific gene).

## Alternative reading
`gap-filling:first-measurement` is a plausible alternative (first measurement of PtoWRKY60's resistance phenotype specifically), but `next-target` captures the pattern more centrally given how directly this mirrors prior single-gene WRKY characterization papers cited in its own reference list.

## Notes for the codebook
Single-gene-family-member characterization papers (very common in plant molecular biology — "next uncharacterized paralog gets the standard pipeline") are a clean, high-volume instance of `gap-filling:next-target` where "target" = one specific gene rather than species/population; worth confirming this reading is intended, since the pipeline reuse here is at the field level (multiple labs use it) rather than strictly the same authors' own prior work.
