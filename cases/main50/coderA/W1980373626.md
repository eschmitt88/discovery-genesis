## Reasoning
The abstract states a specific, previously untested question (road proximity → reproductive success, not just road-kill) and answers it with an entirely off-the-shelf toolkit: microsatellite genotyping, F-statistics, sibship/kin-group reconstruction, and bottleneck tests, all cited from 1995-2009. The 2009 "molecular road ecology" review already frames genetics as the tool for this kind of question, and a 2007 salamander paper and 2010 review already establish road-genetics effects in other taxa. Nothing in the dossier suggests a new instrument or assay — this is an established analytical toolkit turned on a new species/question, so problem-first, `gap-filling:next-target`. Full text showing the introduction's explicit gap statement would raise confidence to high.

## Alternative reading
Could be read as `recombination` (joining road-ecology theory with population-genetics kinship methods), but the 2009 "molecular road ecology" review shows that combination was already an established field-level move before this paper — so it is a next-target application, not a new joining.
