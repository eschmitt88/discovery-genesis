## Reasoning
The dossier's citation contexts contain the authors' own framing sentence, which states the move directly: take an existing ring-theoretic construction (reduced zero-divisor graph, Mulay 2011 / Spiroff-Wickham) and redefine it for posets, "in analogy with" that prior work. This mirrors an already-established generalization pattern in the same literature — ordinary zero-divisor graphs were carried from rings (Beck 1988) to posets (Lu-Wu 2010) five years earlier. Both the method (equivalence-class construction) and the generalization move (ring-to-poset) pre-exist; what's new is applying the specific reduced-graph construction to this specific structure. Problem-first, `gap-filling:next-target` (established construction, new mathematical structure as target). High confidence because the dossier gives direct quoted authorial framing, not just an abstract.

## Alternative reading
Recombination (joining the equivalence-class idea with the poset zero-divisor-graph framework) is a defensible alternative label, but codebook §2 reserves recombination for joining two things "within reach of the same field" that were not previously combined in this way — here the combination pattern (extend zero-divisor construction X from rings to posets) was already demonstrated for the base case, so this reads more as filling in the next instance of a known generalization than a novel joining.

## Notes for the codebook
