## Reasoning
This is the "mature platform on a new pairing" pattern the codebook flags explicitly: WC/C coatings for gears are a well-established research line (multiple cited papers 1999-2014), and the paper's contribution is measuring an untested lubricant/coating combination rather than introducing new coating or lubrication technology. That places it as problem-first / gap-filling:first-measurement (first measurement of load capacity for this specific coating+oil pairing), not means-first, since the oil blend, while formulated "in-house," is not described as a novel capability, just a tailored formulation.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is arguable if the "tailored, low-viscosity oil" itself is the enabling new ingredient (a new formulation the field lacked) rather than incidental — abstract-only evidence can't settle whether the oil chemistry itself is the paper's real contribution or just a vehicle for testing the coating.

## Notes for the codebook
Single-author, single-paper dossiers with no visible prior-work trail make the "authors' own platform" tell (4-8x citation) impossible to check directly; here I inferred platform maturity from the field-wide reference list instead, which is a weaker signal than the codebook's intended tell.
