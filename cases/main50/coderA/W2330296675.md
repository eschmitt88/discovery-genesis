## Reasoning
Both measurement instruments (load cell, FDR sensor) and the general water-retention characterization approach were already established for rockwool in the cited 1991-1998 literature; this paper's contribution is applying that same characterization to a new substrate (coir), which is the gap-filling:next-target pattern. Evidence that would change my mind: a full Korean-language text revealing a newly designed sensor or measurement protocol rather than standard FDR/load-cell use.

## Alternative reading
Could be read as gap-filling:first-measurement if FDR-sensor error on coir specifically had never been quantified before — plausible given the narrow reference list, but I favor next-target since the paper explicitly frames itself as a comparison against the already-characterized rockwool.
