## Reasoning
Both core ingredients — the directed-forgetting paradigm and sex-hormone/cognition research — are independently well established (the former since 1973, the latter with a large literature from the 1990s-2010s), but the reference list shows no prior work joining them for directed forgetting specifically. No new instrument is introduced (standard word lists, standard salivary hormone assays). That is problem-first, `recombination` — two established lines from the same broad field (cognitive/behavioral neuroscience) joined for a specific new question.

## Alternative reading
`gap-filling:next-target` is plausible if directed forgetting had already been studied across some of these hormonal-status subgroups individually and this paper's contribution is mainly extending to the remaining groups — abstract does not indicate this; the four-group cross-comparison design argues for recombination over a simple population extension.
