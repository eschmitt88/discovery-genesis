## Reasoning
This is a strong instance of the codebook's "established platform on the next target" pattern: the paper's own reference list includes a nearly identically titled and structured prior study ("An Examination of How Professionals Who Abstain from Alcohol Communicatively Negotiate Their Non-drinking Identity," 2014) applying the same facework/thematic-interview design to a different non-normative-identity population. That, plus the abstract's explicit citation of the theoretical lens (Goffman 1967; Cupach & Metts 1994) as pre-existing rather than newly proposed, places this problem-first / gap-filling:next-target with high confidence — the method and theory are established, the target population (weight-loss maintainers) is what's new.

## Alternative reading
idea-first is a weak alternative if facework theory's application here is read as testing a new proposition about weight-specific face threats, but the abstract frames the theory as an existing "lens" applied to organize findings rather than a hypothesis stated in advance of data collection, so problem-first remains more likely.
