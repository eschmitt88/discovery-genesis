The 160-reference list is a near-comprehensive sweep of species-specific wine-yeast papers rather than a focused citation trail toward one open question, consistent with the codebook's accretion test: the conclusion's form (non-Saccharomyces yeasts are a valuable, under-used resource) could be stated before any specific numbers were filled in. Evidence would change my mind if full text showed an original dataset (e.g., a new isolate screen) buried in the review.
