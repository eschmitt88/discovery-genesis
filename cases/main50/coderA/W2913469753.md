## Reasoning

To address this knowledge gap...") and answers it with an already-optimized assay (B-cell ELISpot, 2013) previously applied to natural infection and other cholera vaccine formulations in the same research community. No new instrument or assay is introduced — this is the established MBC-measurement approach applied to a specific vaccine formulation and population not yet studied. Problem-first, `gap-filling:next-target`.
