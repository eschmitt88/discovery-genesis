## Reasoning
Unlike the serial-platform-reuse pattern elsewhere in this batch, here the multi-tissue breadth and the RNA-seq+shRNA-screen combination are presented as the specific solution to a named methodological problem (context-dependence masking consistent signal) that the field's prior narrower studies could not solve — this is capability→observation→claim, means-first by the codebook's definition, not just an established assay pointed at a new target. Evidence that would change my mind: if the authors' own prior papers already used this same multi-tissue panel + dual-assay combination (no such self-citation appears in the reference trail read).

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is a plausible alternative if one treats "consistent T21 signature" as simply the next open question in a long-running gene-expression literature, with RNA-seq and shRNA screening as routine, established techniques individually. I favor means-first because the paper's own framing centers the breadth/combination itself as the enabling innovation, not just as tools pointed at a pre-existing question.
