## Reasoning
The abstract presents this as filling in specific, previously uncharacterized nodes (two repressor proteins and their mechanism) within an already well-established regulatory model (the MBW complex), using genetic and molecular methods (transgenics, motif analysis) standard to plant regulatory biology and, per the topic list (Plant Gene Expression Analysis), likely this group's own established petunia platform. This is problem-first: the specific open question (how is anthocyanin transcription repressed, not just activated) is what's new; the tools that answer it are not. gap-filling:first-measurement fits because the paper reports the first identification/characterization of these specific repressor components and their intercellular-movement behavior in this system.

## Alternative reading
Recombination is a plausible alternative primary label if MYB27/MYBx characterization is better read as joining repressor biology from a separate sub-literature with the activator-focused MBW literature — but both repressor and activator work here sit within the same anthocyanin-regulation field (not a field-boundary crossing), so I judged first-measurement the more central, non-redundant label.

## Notes for the codebook
Plant/animal regulatory-network "identify the missing repressor/component" papers are a recurring first-measurement pattern distinct from the more common "established assay on new species" next-target pattern — worth keeping first-measurement's scope broad enough to cover "new component in an established network" cases, not just "new quantity in an established system".
