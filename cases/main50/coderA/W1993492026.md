## Reasoning
The reference list itself gives away the platform reuse: a near-identically named/structured procedure ("Four Convergence Seminars" on nanobiotechnology, 2009) predates this paper by three years, strongly suggesting the same research group's established deliberation platform is being run again on a new technology topic. That is the textbook problem-first / gap-filling:next-target pattern under the v1.1 "capability new to the authors or field" rule — the procedure is not new, the target domain (BMI) is.

## Alternative reading
idea-first is a weak alternative if "hypothetical retrospection" is read as a proposition driving the design, but that framework is itself cited to 2007 prior work, not stated here as this paper's proposition — so it does not meet the idea-first bar (a proposition original to this paper).

## Notes for the codebook
Good illustration of why the "authors' own prior platform, next target" tell matters even off a single reference-list hit (not needing 4-8 citations) — one clearly parallel prior application was enough to resolve this confidently as problem-first rather than means-first.
