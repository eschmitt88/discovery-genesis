## Alternative reading
Could be read as `gap-filling:first-measurement` (first report of these two specific mutations), but the codebook's `next-target` example explicitly covers "new target/population" and case-report genetics like this fits that pattern more centrally than a first-measurement-of-a-quantity framing.

## Notes for the codebook
