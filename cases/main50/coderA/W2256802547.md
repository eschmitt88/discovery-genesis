## Reasoning
This is a standard natural-product bioactivity screening paper: an established, field-wide panel of assays (DPPH, reducing power, total antioxidant activity, NO-inhibition, MTT/A549 cytotoxicity) is applied to a set of solvent extracts from a specific plant material. The reference list shows this exact assay combination used repeatedly across many other plant materials, and a 2011 prior study already characterized Jerusalem artichoke's antioxidant activity under different (methanol-only) conditions — this paper extends that to new solvents and adds anti-inflammatory/cytotoxicity endpoints. Problem-first, gap-filling:next-target: established assay panel, new material/extract-condition target.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is unlikely here since no new assay or instrument is introduced; the only alternative worth naming is that if this specific solvent-extraction protocol were itself novel to the field (not just to this plant), it could edge toward means-first, but the reference list gives no indication of that.
