## Reasoning
The density of closely related self-citations (same reductive-coupling/transfer-hydrogenation strategy applied to alkyne-aldehyde, alkyne-imine, and enyne substrates across a decade of prior papers) is the clearest instance in this batch of the v1.1 problem-first tell. The specific catalyst pairing and substrate class are new, but the underlying mechanistic strategy is not. Evidence that would change my mind: if the introduction described a fundamentally new catalytic mechanism rather than a new substrate/catalyst combination within the same mechanistic family.

## Alternative reading
Means-first would require this specific Ni/Ru divergent-regioselectivity result to be presented as a new capability rather than an application of the existing platform; the abstract instead frames it as achieving a specific outcome (regiocontrol, no exogenous reductant) within the established strategy, arguing against means-first.
