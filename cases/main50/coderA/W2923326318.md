## Reasoning
This is a textbook `gap-filling:bigger-n` case: the same question (does electrode position predict CI outcome) has been studied repeatedly with small, sometimes underpowered samples (explicitly noted in the dossier's own citation contexts), and this paper's contribution is a larger cohort (220 ears, N=85-92 per array/outcome combination) measured with the authors' own already-published automated segmentation tools rather than any new instrument. The title's own framing ("Further Evidence") and the direct quote about small-sample limitations make this a high-confidence problem-first call.

## Alternative reading
`gap-filling:next-target` could apply if the real novelty is the specific new positional variable (modiolar proximity, explicitly flagged in the abstract as "less extensively studied") rather than simply more data — but the "bigger-n" framing dominates given the explicit small-sample-limitation citations and the "Further Evidence" title.
