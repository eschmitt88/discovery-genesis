## Reasoning
The reference list shows this exact template repeated across multiple waste materials (rice husk ash 2004, fly ash 2004, sewage sludge ash 2008, phosphogypsum 2006) all applied to soil-cement stabilization — this paper simply substitutes ultrafine POFA as the next material in that series, matching gap-filling:next-target squarely under codebook v1.1. The underlying stabilization method itself (cement + pozzolanic additive) is over 50 years old, so problem-first via the established-method/new-material rule, not means-first.

## Alternative reading
means-first is possible if "ultrafine" grinding/processing of POFA to a new particle-size regime is itself a novel capability (a new-instrument enabler) rather than routine material characterisation — title+refs-only evidence cannot confirm or rule this out, and the emphasis on "ultrafine" in the title is the main hint against my problem-first reading.
