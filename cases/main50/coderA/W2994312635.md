## Reasoning
The abstract explicitly isolates a specific surgical subgroup (MMCR without concurrent blepharoplasty) as the new target for an established, decades-old preoperative test (phenylephrine testing), addressing a gap left open by prior studies that either combined MMCR with blepharoplasty or did not isolate this specific comparison. This is problem-first, gap-filling:next-target: an established clinical test applied to answer a specific, narrower, previously unaddressed clinical question (does the test still predict outcome when blepharoplasty is not performed).

## Alternative reading
gap-filling:first-measurement is a weaker alternative if this is read as measuring a specific quantity (agreement between predicted and actual tarsal platform show) for the first time in any MMCR population, rather than isolating a subgroup — but the abstract's explicit framing around the "without blepharoplasty" subgroup, contrasted with prior studies that included blepharoplasty, favors next-target as the more central move.
