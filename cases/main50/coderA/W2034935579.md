## Reasoning
The abstract's own framing ("a number of selected, noteworthy industrial examples are discussed to showcase") is a survey/review statement, and the conclusion's form ("catalysis is increasingly important and here are examples") could be written before assembling the specific case list — the codebook §1.1 accretion test. `is_primary: no — review` and `genesis_model: accretion` follow directly; `consolidation` captures the move of gathering many independent catalytic case studies into one industrial-impact narrative.

## Alternative reading
If this paper in fact reports new proprietary process data from the authors' own Boehringer Ingelheim manufacturing work (rather than only surveying published/known industrial examples), it would be closer to problem-first or means-first primary research — abstract-only evidence cannot rule this out, since industrial authors sometimes present their own unpublished process work inside a review-styled article.

## Notes for the codebook
Industrial-review articles from pharma R&D groups are a recognisable genre distinct from academic literature reviews (Kinesio-taping example) — same accretion/consolidation coding applies, but the "authors' own unpublished case included among surveyed examples" ambiguity is a real recurring risk worth a note.
