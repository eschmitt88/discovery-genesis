## Reasoning
The full text explicitly frames the paper's own contribution as methodological ("we present a methodological GIS-based mapping approach"), and the reference list shows what appears to be the same author group's own prior Porto-specific groundwater GIS/vulnerability papers (2016, 2019) — an established local platform being extended (with geovisualisation techniques) and demonstrated on two specific new catchments. This favors problem-first, though the central move is best read as recombination (joining GIS mapping, geovisualisation, and hydrogeomorphological field inventory into one integrated framework) rather than a pure next-target case, since the paper explicitly emphasizes the combination/integration of previously separate technique streams as its contribution, not just a new study area.

## Alternative reading
gap-filling:next-target is a reasonable alternative primary label if the paper is read as simply the established Porto GIS-index methodology applied to two new catchments — I judged recombination slightly more central because the title itself foregrounds "coupling groundwater GIS mapping and geovisualisation techniques" as the methodological contribution, i.e. the integration itself, not just the new location.
