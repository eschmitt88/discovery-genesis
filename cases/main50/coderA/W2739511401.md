## Reasoning
This is a clean instance of the codebook's flagged "mature platform, next target" pattern: the exact same zein-complex-nanoparticle-for-Pickering-emulsion template appears in the reference list run with at least five other polysaccharides/proteins in the two years immediately preceding this paper (2015-2016), and the method (antisolvent precipitation/electrostatic complexation, then standard characterisation) is not described as new. That makes gum arabic the "next target" material rather than a new capability, and the tell (an established platform reused repeatedly across the field, if not necessarily by these exact authors) places this problem-first.

## Alternative reading
recombination is a defensible alternative primary label (joining two specific existing materials, zein and gum arabic, not previously paired) since the zein+GA pairing itself is new — but gap-filling:next-target already captures "established method, new material" more precisely per codebook §2, and the pairing is best read as the next iteration of an active series rather than a novel combination across a field boundary.
