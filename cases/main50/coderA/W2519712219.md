## Reasoning
The abstract explicitly frames this as extending a prior, narrower finding (preserved anterior midcingulate thickness in superagers) to two named, already-established brain networks (default mode, salience), using standard structural-MRI/cortical-thickness methodology (FreeSurfer, cited back to 2000) and network definitions that include what appears to be this group's own prior salience-network paper (Touroutoglou et al. 2012). This is problem-first, gap-filling:next-target: an established measurement platform and established network definitions applied to the next specific target (does preservation extend beyond one region to two whole networks), not a new capability.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a weaker alternative if the specific combination of superager-classification criteria with whole-network (rather than single-region) cortical-thickness analysis is itself judged a new analytic capability — but the abstract's explicit "building on prior research" framing and stated a priori prediction argue for problem-first.
