## Reasoning
The dispatch-with-wind problem itself is well established in the cited literature back to 2006–2008 (multiple PSO-and-wind dispatch papers already exist), so the "new" element is the specific PSO-improvement combination, not the target problem — this is recombination of two named algorithmic strategies within the same field (metaheuristic optimization), applied to a problem the field already studies (problem-first). Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing the solution-sharing or elitist-learning strategy itself was invented in this paper rather than adapted from the cited metaheuristics literature, which would push toward means-first.
