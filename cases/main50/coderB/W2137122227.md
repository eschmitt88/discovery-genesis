## Reasoning
The abstract names the specific limiting assumption of prior lethal-mutagenesis models (infinite N) and states the paper's task as relaxing it — the textbook `relaxation` move, and a clean rule-3 problem-first case since the tools used (population-genetics theory, a biophysics-based fitness landscape) are established, not new. The paper's second-order contribution — showing lethal mutagenesis (mutation-rate-driven) and finite-population drift-driven extinction ("lethal isolation") interact synergistically — is a genuine joining of two previously distinct sub-literatures within evolutionary genetics (drug-induced error catastrophe vs. Muller's-ratchet/mutation-load theory), which is why `recombination` is listed alongside `relaxation` rather than folded into it: removing it would drop the paper's headline synergy finding.

## Alternative reading
`idea-first` is the alternative if "lethal isolation" is read as a freestanding hypothesis stated before any modeling (population size alone can drive extinction, independent of U) that then organizes the paper's design. Judged less likely as primary because the abstract's own logical structure starts from a named gap in existing infinite-N models, not from a proposition stated up front.

## Notes for the codebook
Strong candidate to attest the currently-unattested `relaxation` label: relaxing a named simplifying assumption (infinite N) of an established theoretical model is a clean, recognizable move distinct from `gap-filling` (no new target/population/material — the "target" is the assumption itself) and from `formalisation` (no informal construct is being formalized; an existing formal model is being generalized).
