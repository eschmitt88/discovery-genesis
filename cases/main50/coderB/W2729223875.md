## Reasoning
The title's own phrasing places the fabrication method first ("microfluidic fabrication of photonic encoding magnetized silica microspheres") and the application ("for aptamer-based enrichment of Ochratoxin A") second, suggesting the capability (a new dual-function, optically-encoded magnetic microsphere) is the reason the OTA application exists, matching the means-first decision-order test (capability -> observation/demonstration -> claim). The individual ingredients (Stöber silica synthesis, aptamer-OTA recognition, photonic/magnetic microfluidic particle fabrication) each predate the paper, but their specific combination for OTA is not evidenced elsewhere in the reference list.

## Alternative reading
problem-first (gap-filling:next-target) is a real alternative: if the field already routinely fabricates such photonic-magnetic microspheres and simply swaps the aptamer/analyte pair, this would be "established assay/platform applied to a new target (OTA)" rather than a new capability. Without an abstract or full text, it cannot be determined whether the microsphere fabrication chemistry itself required new development for this paper or was a straightforward reuse of the cited 2013/2015/2016 fabrication recipes; genesis_confidence is kept low specifically because this distinction is exactly what the missing introduction would resolve.
