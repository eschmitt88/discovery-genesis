## Reasoning
With no abstract at all, this card leans entirely on the reference list, which documents a mechanistic debate running from 1953 through at least 2010 (field-assisted dissolution, viscous flow, O18 tracer studies) with no single resolved answer. The title's framing ("the role of...") suggests the paper stakes out a mechanistic position using existing experimental evidence rather than introducing new instrumentation, so problem-first is the more defensible default, but I flag this as genuinely underdetermined. Evidence that would change my mind: an abstract or full text showing a new in-situ or tracer technique introduced specifically for this paper (→ means-first), or a single stated proposition the design follows from (→ idea-first).

## Alternative reading
idea-first is a real alternative — if the paper's contribution is a specific proposition about the field's role (e.g., "the field controls X, not Y") stated up front and then argued for, rather than an open question closed with existing tools, idea-first would apply. Title+refs-only cannot distinguish this from problem-first, which is exactly why confidence is capped at low.
