## Reasoning
The "Conspectus" heading is an unambiguous ACS Accounts of Chemical Research genre marker — these are explicitly retrospective, first-person syntheses of a research programme's recent progress, not primary reports of a single new result, which is a clean accretion case (the conclusion's form — "twisted-amide resonance control enables previously impossible cross-couplings" — could be sketched before compiling the specific examples). move_candidates is consolidation rather than unification, since amide/ester cross-coupling and Pd-NHC catalysis were not "previously separate literatures" shown to be one mechanism — they are the same sub-field's own recent output being synthesized. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible — the genre marker alone is decisive here.

## Alternative reading
No credible alternative genesis model fits an ACS Conspectus; the only real judgment call is is_primary — since Accounts often substantially restate the authors' own primary results, a `partial — review of own work` reading is defensible, but the codebook's genre list treats reviews as `no` regardless of self-citation density, so I followed that.

## Notes for the codebook
ACS "Conspectus"-headed Accounts of Chemical Research articles are a reliable, cheap genre signal (like MMWR/ESPAD for surveillance) that the codebook could name explicitly alongside review/guideline/consensus/editorial as an unambiguous `no — review` marker, since they recur across chemistry dossiers and are easy to misread as primary given how much of the authors' own recent primary work they summarize.
