## Reasoning
The paper names its own gap directly and precisely: mutation status is a well-covered variable (with acknowledged conflicting findings), but expression level had, in the authors' words, "not been investigated." Both assay types used (mutation genotyping, qPCR expression quantification) are established, uncited-as-novel techniques, and the patient cohort is an existing single-institution resource with prior treatment records — nothing new was built to make this measurement possible. That places the paper squarely in problem-first, and the specific move is `gap-filling:first-measurement`: a quantity (expression level as it relates to DTIC response/prognosis) measured for the first time in this system, using tools that already existed.

## Alternative reading
`gap-filling:next-target` was considered (established expression-quantification method applied to yet another cancer-marker/disease combination), but the paper's own framing centers on a specific unmeasured *variable* within an already-well-characterized system (melanoma BRAF/NRAS biology), not a new disease or population — first-measurement is the more precise fit.
