## Reasoning
Both the compound (CBD) and the outcome paradigm (SPST) predate this paper; the authors' own 2010 neuroimaging paper on CBD in SAD signals serial extension of an established research platform to a new (behavioral/RCT) target rather than a new means. Per the v1.1 tell, this authorial continuity across the group's 2003/2009/2010 CBD papers argues for problem-first over means-first. recombination is kept as a second label because the specific move — pairing an anxiety-behavioral paradigm with a pharmacological RCT design in this population — is a genuine within-field combination that would make the card materially incomplete without noting it (SPST was not previously used with CBD by this group).

## Alternative reading
means-first would apply if 600mg CBD dosing or the RCT design itself were a new methodological capability for the group, but nothing in the abstract suggests that; RCT methodology and CBD dosing were already established in prior human CBD studies (1982 and 1993 Zuardi-lineage papers appear in the same reference list).
