## Reasoning
The paper names a specific, narrow documentation gap ("few resources available... to address this deficit") and closes it entirely with existing technology and the authors' own accumulated operational know-how, not a new capability — a clean problem-first case. move_candidates is gap-filling:other because none of the three specific sub-labels fit: it is not a next-target/population application of a method to answer a research question, not a bigger-n study, and not a first measurement of a quantity — it is a practical operating manual filling a documentation void. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible, since the genre and gap are both stated explicitly.

## Alternative reading
accretion is a plausible alternative if this is read purely as "compiling existing tips into a document" with no real gap being closed — but the paper is explicit that the specific artefact (a troubleshooting guide) did not exist before, which is a genuine problem-first closure rather than a survey-of-what-is-known genre piece, so I favor problem-first.

## Notes for the codebook
Practical/operational guides (as distinct from literature reviews or clinical-practice guidelines) are another genre without a clean home in is_primary — `partial — practical guide/protocol` seems right by analogy to `partial — protocol papers`, but the codebook's protocol-paper example language is written with lab protocols in mind, and this is closer to an equipment operating manual grounded in clinical experience rather than a step-by-step method; worth confirming this genre sits under the same umbrella.
