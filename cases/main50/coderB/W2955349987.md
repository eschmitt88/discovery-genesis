## Reasoning
No new instrument or survey method is introduced; the study applies the same basic approach (structured patient interview about unsupervised topical steroid use during a routine OPD visit) used in the cited prior Indian and international hospital-based TS-misuse studies, to a population (this specific hospital's dermatology OPD) not previously surveyed. This matches problem-first / gap-filling:next-target, in the same pattern as other local-first-application studies in this batch (cf. the REGICOR-Paraguay card).

## Alternative reading
accretion is a weak alternative if this were read as a purely descriptive local prevalence snapshot with no analytic claim, but the abstract reports specific risk associations (age group, facial disease as risk factors; gender as a non-factor) with statistical testing (relative risk, P<0.01), which is an analytic contribution beyond simple aggregation, so problem-first is preferred.
