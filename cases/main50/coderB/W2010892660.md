## Reasoning
No new instrument or dataset type is described; the cited toolkit (classical statistics/econometrics texts) is old and general-purpose. The recurring pattern of names implicitly tied to prior electricity-price-risk econometrics papers (efficient frontier, cross-hedging, Pacific Northwest) matches the v1.1 "tell" for problem-first: an established analytic platform serially applied to a new target (Texas/ERCOT instead of the Pacific Northwest or Germany). The specific question (Texas price/variance impact) is pinned recent by several 2009-2011 ERCOT-specific citations.

## Alternative reading
means-first is possible if the real "ingredient" is the ERCOT dataset itself only recently becoming large enough to analyze (wind penetration in Texas grew rapidly 2008-2011) — then the capability would be "enough wind-penetration data," closer to new-dataset-or-resource. But since the analytic method is old and general and the authors appear to reuse it across markets, problem-first (next-target) is the better fit under the v1.1 tiebreaker.
