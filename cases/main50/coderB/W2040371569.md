## Reasoning
This is an unambiguous means-first case: the abstract's own language ("first... demonstrate," "a new mode-locking method, which we term...") explicitly frames a newly introduced instrument/mechanism as the reason the finding (a stable, narrow-linewidth, ultrahigh-repetition-rate mode-locked laser) exists. The citation contexts confirm the new mechanism (FD-FWM) is a genuine adaptation, not a repackaging, of prior fibre-laser dissipative-FWM mode-locking (1998/2002) to a structurally different platform (an integrated CMOS-compatible microring resonator rather than a fibre cavity), and the paper's own claimed advantage (narrower linewidth) is derived directly from this new platform's physics. `Instrument` is the single move label needed — the paper's core content is the introduction and demonstration of this new capability, and no second label was needed to keep the card true.

## Alternative reading
`Recombination` was considered (joining the separately-developed threads of high-Q resonator combs and mode-locked lasing), since the introduction explicitly motivates the work by pointing at recent resonator-comb demonstrations. This was set aside as primary because both prior threads are within the same field (nonlinear/laser optics) and the paper's decisive move is inventing a new mechanism (FD-FWM) to make the combination physically work, which is better captured as `instrument` than as a same-field recombination of pre-existing techniques.
