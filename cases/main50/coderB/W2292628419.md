## Reasoning
The abstract is unusually explicit about the specific unsatisfactory prior states this paper closes (poor adhesion, chemical susceptibility, a solar-modulation/transparency tradeoff, and Ti4+ diffusion degrading VO2 in the existing VO2+TiO2 combination), and the fix — an SiO2 barrier layer — is a well-established thin-film material, deposited via the same APCVD technique the reference list shows this group using across a decade of prior VO2/TiO2 window-coating papers (2003-2013). That is a clean rule-3 problem-first case with the v1.1 tell present (established platform, cited repeatedly in the field/group's own prior work, applied here by adding one more established component). `recombination` fits since VO2, SiO2, and TiO2 are three independently established materials/technologies joined in a new layered configuration within the same field (thin-film materials science).

## Alternative reading
`means-first` is the alternative if the specific layered deposition process ("quickly deposited," implying a faster or otherwise qualitatively different APCVD protocol) constitutes a genuinely new capability rather than a routine application of established APCVD. Judged less likely because the abstract frames APCVD itself as "an ideal technology" already available and well-suited to the task, not as something newly developed in this paper.

## Notes for the codebook
Materials-science papers with abstracts that explicitly enumerate multiple named prior-art limitations (adhesion, chemical susceptibility, translucency tradeoff, interlayer diffusion) followed by a specific fix are a strong and easy-to-spot problem-first signature, distinct from the harder means-first/problem-first calls elsewhere in this batch.
