## Reasoning
The paper's own framing ("as a first step in establishing a link between acetaminophen use and neuronal apoptosis") names a specific, recently opened question rather than a decades-old one, and the assay used to answer it (cortical neuron viability culture) is standard toxicology methodology, not introduced here. That fits problem-first with gap-filling:first-measurement, since the toxicity of this specific metabolite in developing neurons had apparently not been measured before. Evidence that would shift this to means-first: if full text showed a new viability/apoptosis assay specific to CB1 pathway readouts was developed for this paper.

## Alternative reading
idea-first is a weaker alternative — one could read "the mechanism of cell death could be through the cannabinoid receptors" as a stated hypothesis — but this is a post hoc interpretive claim built on the anandamide comparison result, not a proposition stated up front that the design follows from, so I do not rate it as likely.

## Notes for the codebook
This is a clean case of a paper that exists because an earlier mechanistic finding (2005) opened a new, narrow safety question — the "problem" is only ~2–6 years old even though the drug (acetaminophen) and general field are over a century old; problem_age_specific handles this well but is worth flagging as a common biomedical pattern (old drug, new mechanism, new narrow safety question).
