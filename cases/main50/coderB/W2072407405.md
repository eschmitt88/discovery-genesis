## Reasoning
The dossier's citation-context excerpts make this an unusually well-evidenced case despite lacking the fetched full text: the authors state outright that they are working "in analogy with" two ring-theoretic papers (Mulay 2002, Spiroff & Wickham 2011), applying an already-existing construction to a new mathematical structure (posets, whose non-reduced zero-divisor graph, Lu & Wu 2010, already existed). This is a textbook `gap-filling:next-target` — established method (the reduced-graph construction), new target (posets instead of rings) — decided under problem-first per the decision order, since no new capability is introduced and the ingredient (the construction) predates the question addressed.
