## Reasoning
The paper explicitly targets an existing, mature, routinely-used assay (SYTO9/PI, established 1996) and states its goal as critical evaluation rather than introducing a new capability — the microplate reader and CLSM are routine lab instruments, not novel. Per the decision order, means-first is ruled out because no capability new to the authors or field drives the finding; the paper instead closes a stated open question (assay reliability was previously "not critically evaluated") using existing tools — problem-first. It is a first systematic quantitative characterization on this platform, hence gap-filling:first-measurement rather than next-target (no serial "next organism" framing — the two organisms are used together within one study to establish general artefacts, not as successive targets of a platform).

## Alternative reading
instrument could be argued as a secondary move if the paper is read as contributing a new evaluation protocol for validating live/dead kits — but the paper does not present a new protocol as its contribution, only quantified findings about an existing one, so instrument was dropped per the "removing it would make the card false" rule.
