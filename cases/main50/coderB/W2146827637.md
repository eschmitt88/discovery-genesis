## Reasoning
Every methodological component here — WRKY family characterization (phylogenetics, expression profiling), Agrobacterium-mediated poplar transformation, and pathogen-challenge assays — predates this paper by years and recurs in the same research group's own prior publications on transgenic Populus tomentosa disease resistance (2010, 2012). That is precisely the v1.1 tell for problem-first over means-first: an established platform reused on the next gene (PtoWRKY60) and pathogen (D. gregaria) rather than a capability introduced here. `gap-filling:next-target` captures this directly — established method, new target (this specific WRKY paralog / this specific pathogen).

## Alternative reading
`Recombination` (joining WRKY signaling biology with transgenic disease-resistance engineering) was considered but rejected as a second label: both sub-fields are already routinely combined in this exact literature (cf. the 2009 PtoWRKY23 paper doing the same thing), so labeling it recombination would not be removing a true statement so much as restating gap-filling from a different angle.

## Notes for the codebook
A recurring genus in plant molecular biology: "characterize ortholog X in species Y, overexpress, challenge with pathogen Z" is functionally identical in structure to the biomedical "next gene in next cancer type" next-target pattern — worth a plant-science worked example alongside the biomedical ones already in the codebook.
