## Reasoning
This is a textbook case of the v1.1 rule: the sequencing/annotation/phylogenetic pipeline is cited to (and matches almost word-for-word) at least four prior papers on other Geometridae species, and the paper's contribution is explicitly "the new representative of the mitogenome of the subfamily" — a next-target application, not a new capability. is_primary is `partial` because the contribution is fundamentally a deposited genomic resource (GenBank accession) rather than a novel finding, per the codebook's dataset/resource-paper guidance. Evidence that would change my mind: none plausible here — full text is unambiguous.

## Alternative reading
means-first has no real support: no new sequencing technology, kit, or analysis method is introduced (QIAamp DNA kit and standard Bayesian PCG-matrix phylogenetics are both off-the-shelf); the only new-here element is the specimen and resulting sequence data itself.

## Notes for the codebook
Clean, unambiguous worked example for the "capability new to the authors" rule outside biomedicine — the repeated four-citation string is as strong a textual tell as the codebook's own example. Also a clean is_primary case: genomic single-species mitogenome papers are "partial — dataset/resource" even when full text and genesis_model confidence are both high, since the artefact (not a finding) is the actual contribution.
