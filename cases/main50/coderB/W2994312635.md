## Reasoning
Both the diagnostic test (phenylephrine testing) and the surgical procedure (MMCR) are established for decades; the paper's contribution is isolating a specific, previously-unmeasured subgroup (MMCR performed alone, without the confound of concurrent blepharoplasty) using a standard retrospective chart-review design. That is rule-3 problem-first with `gap-filling:first-measurement` as the move, since the paper measures a specific quantity (test-to-outcome correlation) for the first time in this isolated clinical subgroup.

## Alternative reading
`means-first` is not plausible — no new instrument or measurement technique is introduced; the computer-software tarsal-platform-show measurement is a routine clinical-photography analysis tool. `idea-first` is a weak alternative only if read as testing a stated proposition (that removing the blepharoplasty confound would reveal good predictability); judged less likely because the abstract frames the study as a straightforward retrospective correlation analysis rather than a proposition-driven design.
