## Reasoning
Every assay named in the abstract (DPPH, reducing power, total antioxidant capacity, NO-inhibition, MTT-based cytotoxicity) is a decades-old, textbook technique (1983-2001), and a 2011 reference already establishes JA tuber's antioxidant activity specifically — so this paper's contribution is extending an existing screening battery to endpoints (anti-inflammatory, A549 cytotoxicity) not previously tested on this material, the clean rule-3 problem-first pattern with `gap-filling:next-target` as the move.

## Alternative reading
`idea-first` is the weak alternative if the paper explicitly hypothesized a specific mechanism (e.g., a particular phenolic-compound-driven pathway) before testing. Judged unlikely because the abstract describes a routine multi-assay screening design with no stated proposition driving the choice of extracts or assays.
