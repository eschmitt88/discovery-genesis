## Reasoning
The title format ("Two novel mutations in the LOR gene in three families...") and a reference list consisting entirely of prior LOR-mutation case reports spanning 1996–2013 is the clearest possible signature of the v1.1 problem-first tell: an established assay (LOR sequencing) reused serially across the field, here on new patient families, to extend a known mutation catalogue rather than to answer a newly framed question. This is `gap-filling:next-target` per the codebook's explicit definition ("established method, new target/population... includes ... translation" — here the "target" is simply the next set of families).

## Alternative reading
Means-first would require the mutation-screening method itself to be new to these authors or to the field; nothing here suggests that — LOR sequencing for keratoderma has been routine since 1996. Not a serious contender.
