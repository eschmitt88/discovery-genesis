## Reasoning
The load-carrying-capacity gear test and WC/C coating are both established (cited back to 1999–2003); no new instrument or assay is introduced in the abstract. This is the "mature platform applied to next target/combination" pattern the v1.1 rule assigns to problem-first — here the "next target" is a specific coating/oil pairing not previously benchmarked against the carburised-gear baseline, closer to next-target than to bigger-n or first-measurement since the quantity itself (load-carrying capacity) is routinely measured in this literature. Evidence that would move me to means-first: if full text showed the in-house oil formulation itself required developing new tribochemical characterisation not present in prior gear-coating studies.

## Alternative reading
instrument (as a move label) is a weaker secondary candidate if the "oil blend formulated in-house" is treated as a novel enabling ingredient rather than incidental, but the abstract treats the oil as one arm of a comparison, not as a technology being introduced, so it is not listed as a second label.

## Notes for the codebook
Single-author, no-full-text engineering benchmarking papers like this are hard to place on the "authors' own prior work reused" tell, since the dossier's reference list can't show self-citation counts — the closed vocabulary in the codebook doesn't have a way to flag "tell inapplicable, inferred from field-level reuse instead."
