## Reasoning
This is close to the codebook's paradigm case for the v1.1 rule: a mature clinical-trial platform (the GOG single-arm Phase II design, reused since at least 1996) is applied to a new drug/disease-site pairing. Both halves of the "new" combination — bevacizumab and endometrial cancer — individually predate the study by years (bevacizumab already tested in two related gynecologic cancers by the same group; endometrial cancer already the subject of ~20 prior GOG Phase II single-agent trials). No new capability is introduced; the trial design, endpoints, and biomarker assays (IHC, ELISA) are all standard.

## Alternative reading
means-first would require bevacizumab or the trial design itself to be new to this group, which the reference list rules out. idea-first is not supported either — there is no stated mechanistic proposition unique to endometrial cancer that organizes the design beyond "VEGF-A-targeted therapy should work here too, as it does elsewhere."

## Notes for the codebook
Clean confirmatory example of gap-filling:next-target for the specific pattern of a cooperative group's serial single-agent Phase II program — the "target" here is a drug-disease pairing rather than a gene/species/material, which the codebook's worked examples don't explicitly enumerate but which the underlying logic (established platform, new combination, no new capability) covers cleanly.
