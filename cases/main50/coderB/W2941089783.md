## Reasoning
The paper's core index (IPI-Urban) and conceptual-site-model framework are explicitly self-attributed to the authors' own prior publications ("Adapted from Freitas et al.", "Adapted from Chaminé et al."), and the paper's stated focus ("focus on methodology") is on demonstrating how these established pieces couple with GIS mapping and geovisualization techniques on a new case-study catchment — the established-platform-reused-on-a-new-target pattern the v1.1 tell assigns to problem-first. `recombination` captures the joining of several established, separately-developed methodological components (index, conceptual-site-model framework, GIS/geovisualization) into one integrated approach; `gap-filling:next-target` captures the reuse of that established approach on a new specific catchment (Porto's Ribeira da Granja and Massarelos basins) not previously mapped this way.

## Alternative reading
`means-first` is the alternative if the geovisualization techniques themselves are read as a genuinely new capability for this group rather than an established field-level trend. Judged less likely because the text frames geovisualization/GIS integration as "a new emphasis [that] has arisen" in the broader field, not as something introduced by this specific paper.

## Notes for the codebook
