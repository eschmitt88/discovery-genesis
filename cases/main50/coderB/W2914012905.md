## Reasoning
This is a textbook `gap-filling:next-target` case: the GIS+AHP weighted-overlay methodology for groundwater potential mapping is not new — the reference list documents its application, in essentially the same form, to more than a dozen distinct river basins and terrains across several countries since the mid-1990s, and the citation context confirms this paper's own weighted-overlay equation is drawn directly from a 2010 prior study. The only new element is the specific study area (an unmapped basin in the Southern Western Ghats), matching the codebook's "established method, new target" definition precisely and the v1.1 problem-first tell (a method cited repeatedly across the field's own prior work, here applied to the next location).

## Alternative reading
Means-first was briefly considered if the AHP weighting scheme or thematic-layer selection were genuinely novel to this study, but the citation context shows the core weighted-overlay equation is explicitly imported from prior work, ruling this out.
