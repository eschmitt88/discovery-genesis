## Reasoning
The paper's stated aim is exactly a reliability gap-fill: two existing questionnaires, whose test-retest reliability had never been measured, assessed with established statistics (ICC, Bland-Altman, both predating this paper by well over a decade). No new instrument or capability appears anywhere in the design — rule-3 problem-first, `gap-filling:first-measurement` as the move (a specific quantity, test-retest reliability, measured for the first time for these instruments).

## Alternative reading
`means-first` is not plausible here — no new measurement capability is introduced. `idea-first` is a weak alternative only if the specific 21-55 day retest interval were framed as testing a stated proposition about attitude stability over time; the paper instead frames the interval as a practical design choice for a standard reliability study, not a hypothesis-driven one.
