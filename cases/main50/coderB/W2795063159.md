## Reasoning
The citation-context evidence is unusually direct here: the allele-bias threshold criterion is explicitly borrowed ("Per the criteria established by Gendrel et al...") and the hnRNP H binding assay reuses a positive control from an unrelated retroviral-RNA paper — both signal that the paper's methods are assembled from existing RNA-biology toolkits rather than newly invented. The standing question — Isoform 2's function — is named explicitly as unknown in the abstract, and this paper answers it. That is problem-first under the decision order. The card lists two `gap-filling` sub-labels because two genuinely distinct closures are reported: extending an established allele-bias assay to a new gene (`next-target`) and determining a specific, previously-unmeasured function/mechanism for the first time (`first-measurement`); removing either would drop one of the two headline contributions.

## Alternative reading
Means-first was considered because of the mechanistic depth (hnRNP H binding, competing polyadenylation), but every individual assay is citation-supported as pre-existing; the novelty is in what question they were pointed at (IL12RB1 Isoform 2), not in a new capability, so problem-first remains the better fit.
