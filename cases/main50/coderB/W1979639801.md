## Reasoning
The abstract's own moves ("multiple studies demonstrate...", "most international recommendations propose...") are review/aggregation language, not a novel empirical or theoretical claim, and the paper could have been outlined ("recommend X framework, cite the evidence for it") before any new work was done — the accretion test from codebook §1. move_candidates is consolidation: existing theory + evidence gathered into a coherent guideline, nothing recombined across fields or transferred from outside medicine. Evidence that would change my mind: full text showing new original survey data collected by the authors themselves rather than cited literature.

## Alternative reading
problem-first is a weaker alternative if this is read as answering "how should primary-care clinicians counsel on lifestyle change" with the 5-A's/motivational-interviewing toolkit as the pre-existing ingredient — but the paper doesn't test or apply that toolkit to new data, it recommends it, which is accretion/consolidation rather than an evidenced answer to an open question.
