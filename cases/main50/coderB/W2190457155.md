## Reasoning
The experimental design that does the real work here — chemostat cultures that decouple temperature from growth rate — is cited to the authors' own prior (2014) paper using the same decoupling strategy on a different question (general CHO cell behavior), and the ERAD-pathway pharmacological tools are all established from the 1977–2007 literature. This matches the v1.1 pattern of an established platform reused on a next, more specific target (ER processing/degradation of one particular recombinant protein, rht-PA) rather than a new capability — problem-first, gap-filling:next-target. Evidence that would change my mind: if the ERAD-pathway inhibitor panel or the chemostat protocol itself had been newly developed for this study rather than drawn from cited prior work.

## Alternative reading
gap-filling:first-measurement is a plausible secondary reading (first time ERAD-pathway sensitivity is measured for rht-PA specifically, and first time temperature vs. growth-rate effects are decoupled for ER processing specifically) — I did not add it as a second label since next-target already captures the "established design, new specific target" logic without needing both labels to make the card true.
