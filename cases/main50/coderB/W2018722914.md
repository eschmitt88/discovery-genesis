## Reasoning
This is close to the codebook's own worked example: "a mature assay/platform applied to the next target — next gene, next cancer type, next lattice geometry, next species" — here, next steel grade. REM addition is cited across 2000-2013 for cast steel, stainless steel, low-carbon steel, rail steel, and D2 tool steel; H13 is simply the next material in that sequence. No new instrument, dataset, or theory is introduced (OM/SEM/TEM are routine characterization tools).

## Alternative reading
means-first would apply if REM addition to H13 specifically required a new capability (e.g. a novel casting/addition process). The abstract doesn't suggest this — it describes only compositional variation and standard microscopy, so problem-first is favored.
