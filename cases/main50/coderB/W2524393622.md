## Reasoning
The abstract's own framing ("brief overview... throughout Christian history") is the accretion signature per codebook §1: the conclusion's form (a survey of positions across periods) could have been written before the specific sources were assembled, with only the particular examples filled in. There is no stated open question this article closes, so `problem` is written as "none stated" per §7 rather than manufacturing a gap. The move is `consolidation` — gathering and synthesizing a body of existing scholarship and primary sources into one coherent narrative, not introducing new sources, data, or an original argumentative thesis that organizes a design.
