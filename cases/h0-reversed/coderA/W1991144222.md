## Reasoning
Idea-first fails: the paper's own framing is explicitly that of identifying an unexamined variable ("has not been investigated"), not a stated proposition about how the world is. Means-first fails equally clearly, since qPCR expression quantification is routine and not novel to the authors, who had already genotyped this same cohort in prior work. Problem-first is the clean fit: a specific, narrow, explicitly-flagged gap in an otherwise heavily studied area (mutation status vs. expression level as distinct predictive factors) is closed with standard methods on an already-existing resource (the authors' own cohort).

## Alternative reading
No serious alternative. This is analogous to the test-retest reliability paper and the cholera MBC paper in this batch — narrow, well-flagged gap-filling using existing tools on an existing resource.

## Notes for the codebook
A useful example of gap-filling:other where the "gap" is a specific unmeasured molecular variable (expression level, as opposed to the far more studied mutation status) within an already well-trodden research area — distinct from next-target/bigger-n/first-measurement in that neither the population nor the system is new, only the specific measured quantity is.
