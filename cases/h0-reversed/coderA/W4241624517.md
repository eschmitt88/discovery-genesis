## Reasoning
Idea-first does not hold under any hearing order — there is no stated proposition, only a design objective and an achieved record result. The contest is means-first vs. problem-first. I favor means-first because the abstract credits a *newly derived* theoretical tool (the bend-aware crosstalk approximation model) as the mechanism that produced the design, which is squarely the codebook's means-first pattern (a new formalism → a specific finding). Trench-assisted core design and pure-silica fabrication are pre-existing ingredients, but the paper's own emphasis is on the new model as the reason the specific combination of low crosstalk and low attenuation could be simultaneously achieved and verified.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is plausible if one treats "achieve low-crosstalk, low-attenuation MCF" as the pre-existing open question and treats trench-assisted design + pure-silica fabrication as already-existing ingredients simply combined and optimized — the new model could then be read as incidental analytical support rather than the core enabler. I recorded this as a tie.
