## Reasoning
Idea-first fails under the reversed hearing: the introduction's own language ("we now evaluated whether...") is explicitly an investigative question, and nothing in the design follows from a proposition stated before the work. Means-first also fails — every technique used (CGH, SSI grading, expression microarrays, IPA, proteomics) predates this paper by years and is applied largely as validated elsewhere (the SSI method is explicitly imported from a 2004 breast-cancer paper, a between-cancer-type "next-target" application within the same broad field). Problem-first is the clear fit: a specific, well-posed comparative question (instability grade vs. histology as the better correlate of expression change) is answered with pre-existing tools on a defined, well-characterized cohort.

## Alternative reading
Accretion was briefly considered given the multi-layer "profiling" character of the study (cytometry + CGH + transcriptomics + proteomics stacked together), which can read as data aggregation. But the paper performs genuine new correlative analysis and hypothesis-testing on original patient samples rather than aggregating pre-existing published results, so problem-first fits the codebook's definitions better.

## Notes for the codebook
A useful example of "gap-filling:next-target" operating at the level of an analytical method transplanted between cancer types (SSI: breast → endometrial) rather than the more common "next species/population" pattern — worth noting alongside the poset math paper as another domain instance of the same underlying move.
