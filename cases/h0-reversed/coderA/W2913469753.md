## Reasoning
Idea-first fails cleanly under the reversed hearing: the paper states an explicit "knowledge gap" and measurement objective, not a proposition organizing a novel design. Means-first also fails — every assay used is established, previously validated, and explicitly cited as "as previously described," strongly suggesting the authors' own or closely related groups' prior methodology reused on a new vaccine/population target. Problem-first is the clear, well-evidenced fit: a specific, narrow, recently-relevant question (does this particular vaccine formulation induce MBC responses) is closed with off-the-shelf immunological assays.

## Alternative reading
None of the other three labels is competitive. This is one of the more straightforward problem-first cases in the batch.

## Notes for the codebook
Another clean instance of gap-filling:next-target where the "target" is a specific vaccine formulation × population combination rather than a species, region, or age group — worth noting as yet another variant of the same underlying pattern recurring across this batch's biomedical papers.
