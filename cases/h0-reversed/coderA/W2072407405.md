## Reasoning
Even asking idea-first first, there is nothing to find: the paper's motivation is a direct analogy to two cited constructions (rings → reduced ZD-graph; posets → unreduced ZD-graph), and the "gap" is combinatorial completeness of the existing taxonomy of constructions, not a proposition about mathematical reality stated in advance. This is the purest form of gap-filling:next-target in mathematics — the "target" being a structure class (posets) rather than a species or population, but the pattern is identical: an established construction (reduced ZD-graph) applied to a new object class where the analogous unreduced construction was already precedent.

## Alternative reading
Recombination is a defensible secondary move-label (joining the "reduced graph" idea with "poset zero-divisor theory," both within the same field/subfield of algebra), but the genesis model itself is unambiguously problem-first — no capability was introduced, and no proposition organizes the design.

## Notes for the codebook
Pure-math "extend construction X, already known for object-class A, to object-class B" papers are a very clean, high-volume instance of gap-filling:next-target; the codebook could usefully note this pattern explicitly alongside the biomedical/next-species examples already given, since mathematics readers might otherwise be tempted toward idea-first on the strength of "novel definition."
