## Reasoning
This is textbook gap-filling:next-target under v1.1: a mature, externally validated instrument (REGICOR) is applied by two authors, neither of whom appears associated with its development, to a population it had never been used on. The discussion section explicitly frames the paper as a comparison against other national/regional applications of the same or similar scales (Spain, Brazil, Colombia). Even under the reversed cascade — asking idea-first first — there is no proposition anywhere in the introduction that could have been written before the work: the paper states a measurement objective, not a claim. Means-first fails cleanly since the instrument long predates this study and is used off-the-shelf. That leaves problem-first as the only label with a clear "yes."

## Alternative reading
Accretion is the only other plausible reading (a routine local surveillance-style report), but the paper analyzes a specific defined cohort with inferential comparisons to the literature rather than aggregating pre-existing records into a trend/standard — so `is_primary: yes` (not `no — surveillance report`) is the better call, and problem-first the better genesis label.

## Notes for the codebook
Clean, unambiguous case for the reversed-cascade check: with idea-first considered first, there is genuinely nothing resembling a proposition to find, so the reversal doesn't create any temptation to over-assign idea-first here.
