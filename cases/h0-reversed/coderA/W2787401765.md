## Reasoning
Idea-first fails outright — there is no proposition, only a stated psychometric measurement gap. Means-first fails equally clearly since both the instruments and the statistical methods are long-established and used exactly as intended by their original developers. Problem-first is the clean, well-evidenced fit: a specific, narrow, explicitly-named property (test-retest reliability) had simply never been measured for these translated instruments, and this paper closes that gap with standard tools.

## Alternative reading
No other label is competitive. This is among the most straightforward problem-first cases in the batch — a pure psychometric-validation gap-fill.

## Notes for the codebook
Another clean instance of "gap-filling:other" — measuring a specific psychometric property (test-retest reliability) not previously assessed for an existing instrument — distinct from next-target, bigger-n, and first-measurement in that the "gap" is a property of the instrument itself rather than of a population or system being studied with it.
