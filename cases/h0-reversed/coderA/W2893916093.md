## Reasoning
Idea-first does not survive the reversed hearing: the paper states an analytic aim, not a proposition, and the biomarker-combination strategy being tested was already established by prior work (including the authors' own). Means-first fails cleanly since both assays are commercially standard and previously validated, including by this same research group in a closely related population. Problem-first is well-evidenced and specific: elderly patients are named explicitly as an understudied/inconclusive subgroup, and the design directly extends the authors' own prior combined-marker approach (self-cited, 2016) to this new age-defined target.

## Alternative reading
No other label is close. Means-first was the only real alternative considered, but the self-citation of the identical approach in a 2016 companion paper by the same authors rules it out under the v1.1 serial-reuse rule.

## Notes for the codebook
Clean "next-target" case where the target is an age subgroup rather than a species, region, or disease — worth noting as another variant of gap-filling:next-target alongside next-population, next-region, and next-cancer-type examples already surfacing in this batch.
