## Reasoning
Q_problem is the clear "yes," well documented by the Background's explicit lineage from the group's own prior, smaller comparative analysis (17 genomes) to this larger one (44 genomes plus new related-species genomes), closing a specifically named open question (dispensable-gene distribution and its relationship to clonal structure). Q_means is "no" under the v1.1 self-citation/own-platform rule: the pan-genome comparative framework is cited to the authors' own earlier work, reapplied here at greater scale and breadth rather than introduced as a new capability. Q_idea is "no" — the paper poses a question rather than asserting a proposition in advance. Q_accretion is "no" despite the paper's large scale — it generates substantial new primary sequence data (24 new S. pneumoniae genomes, new Mitis-group genomes) and new comparative findings, not an aggregation of others' prior published results. One clean "yes" leaves the reversed order nothing to arbitrate.

## Alternative reading
No serious alternative reading. Means-first would require crediting the pan-genome framework itself as new, but the text is explicit that this is the group's own established approach, previously published on a smaller scale.

## Notes for the codebook
