## Reasoning
Q_problem is the clean "yes," strongly supported by the title's own framing as incremental confirmation/extension ("Further Evidence of...") and by the explicit self-citation ("previously developed automated algorithms") that the v1.1 rule specifically identifies as the tell for problem-first over means-first: this is the authors' own established platform reapplied, here to a bigger sample (220 ears, precurved vs. straight arrays analyzed separately) to settle a named inconsistency in the literature (insertion depth's effect). Q_means is "no" precisely because of that self-citation signal — no capability new to the authors or field is introduced. Q_idea is "no" — no proposition precedes the analysis; it is a multivariable statistical investigation. Q_accretion is "no" — this is a bounded primary analysis, not an aggregation.

## Alternative reading
No strong alternative reading presents itself. Means-first would require treating the "automated algorithms" as newly introduced capability, but the abstract's own language ("previously developed") forecloses that.
