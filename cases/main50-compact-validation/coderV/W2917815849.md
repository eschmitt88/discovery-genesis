## Reasoning
The paper's own framing is explicit: a new large-scale, bioinformatics-guided biochemical screening approach (not previously used at this scale by the authors or, apparently, the field) is what directly produces the central findings — the means-first, capability-to-observation-to-claim structure. The cyclic-trinucleotide discovery is a genuine anomaly surfacing from within that systematic screen, distinct from the paper's main planned hypothesis test.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is plausible if one credits the authors' own long DncV/cGAS research programme (8 self-citations) as simply extending its established question (what does this enzyme family do) with routine biochemistry, but the scale and systematicity of the screen (>5,600 bioinformatically identified candidates, 66 purified and tested) is explicitly new relative to any prior single-enzyme study, favoring means-first.
