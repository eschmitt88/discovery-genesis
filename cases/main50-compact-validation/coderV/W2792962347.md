## Reasoning
The self-citation lineage shows the authors already applying Cre-driver optogenetics to the preBötC in their own 2016 and 2017 papers, so the capability is established for this group rather than new — the v1.1 tell for problem-first. The specific open question (how does inhibition, known to be abundant but functionally debated, interact with the intrinsic refractory period the authors themselves had just characterized) was created by the authors' own prior finding and closed here with the same toolkit.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a plausible surface reading given the heavy optogenetics/electrophysiology framing typical of technique-driven neuroscience papers, but the self-citation pattern shows the specific optogenetic approach predates this paper in the same lab, so problem-first (serial reuse to answer the next mechanistic question) fits the v1.1 rule better.

## Notes for the codebook
Optogenetics papers are a recurring hard case for the means-first/problem-first boundary because the technique itself still reads as "high-tech"; the self-citation density here was decisive and is a good candidate worked example for the codebook alongside the existing means-first examples.
