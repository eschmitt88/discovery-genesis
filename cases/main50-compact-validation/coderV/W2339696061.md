## Reasoning
This is a straightforward local application of an already-validated risk-scoring instrument (REGICOR) to a population it had not previously been used on, with zero self-citation and a discussion built entirely around comparing local prevalence figures to other countries' published cohorts — a clean gap-filling:next-target case (established tool, new specific population/setting) rather than any new capability.

## Alternative reading
Accretion is a weak alternative if one reads this as essentially a routine surveillance-style local health report, but the paper frames itself as an original observational study with its own sample and specific local conclusions (recommending routine REGICOR use in this hospital), so problem-first fits better than accretion.

## Notes for the codebook
Small local descriptive/cross-sectional studies applying an internationally validated scale to a specific clinic population are common in regional medical journals; this is a clean, low-ambiguity next-target example that could anchor the codebook's distinction between this pattern and accretion/surveillance reports.
