## Reasoning
The reference list reads as a tightly focused, near-complete lineage of the same FSS-embedded-magnetic-RAM design genre (double-square loops 2007, FSS incorporation 2010, resistive FSS 2012, perforated composite 2014), with this paper's contribution being one more specific unit-cell geometry (treble-square loops) rather than a new absorption mechanism or material — the clean next-target signature.

## Alternative reading
Formalisation is a weak alternative if the paper's simulation-experiment validation loop (HFSS modeling matched to measurement) is read as introducing a new predictive design tool, but HFSS-based simulation of FSS absorbers is itself standard practice in this literature (implied by the cited 2002 "microgenetic algorithm" design-optimization paper), so next-target better describes the core move.
