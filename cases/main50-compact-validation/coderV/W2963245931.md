## Reasoning
This is a textbook next-target case: an established, near-templated sequencing-and-phylogenetics pipeline (visible across the cited 2009-2018 single-species mitogenome papers) applied to one previously unsequenced species, with zero self-citation indicating the authors are simply the next lab in an ongoing field-wide data-collection wave rather than extending their own platform. ## Alternative reading
Accretion is a plausible alternative reading since the paper's conclusion form (genome length, gene content, AT-bias, phylogenetic consistency) could largely have been written before sequencing with only the numbers left blank, but this paper generates genuinely new primary sequence data for a new organism rather than aggregating existing literature, so problem-first/gap-filling:next-target is the better fit per the codebook's explicit "new species" example.
