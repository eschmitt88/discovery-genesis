## Reasoning
The single self-citation is to the authors' own indirect mutation-rate methodology (2006), not to prior sequencing work, indicating whole-genome sequencing is a capability newly adopted for this study rather than the authors' established platform — this is the means-first signature. The abstract's findings (strand-specific transition biases, sequence-context hotspots) are exactly the kind of fine-grained result only attainable once full-genome sequencing of MA lines became feasible, matching the capability-to-observation-to-claim logic of means-first.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is plausible if one reads the MA-line experimental design itself (an established, decades-old approach in this field) as the key ingredient and sequencing as incidental, but the abstract's novel granular claims (leading/lagging-strand-specific biases, sequence-context hotspots) could not have been produced by the pre-sequencing version of this same experimental design, so means-first is the better fit.

## Notes for the codebook
This is a case the v1.1 self-citation rule was built for: an established experimental paradigm (MA lines, in the field since 1991) paired with a newly adopted enabling technology (WGS) from a different sub-lineage of the authors' own work — the single non-sequencing SELF citation is exactly the "0-1 self-citations" tell for means-first.
