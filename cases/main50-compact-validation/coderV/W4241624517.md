## Reasoning
The abstract states directly that a new approximation model was derived to relate crosstalk to design parameters, and that this model then guided the design that was fabricated and measured — a clean capability-to-observation-to-claim structure that is the defining means-first pattern. The short, non-self-citing reference list (0 of 9 SELF) rules out this being the authors' own established platform reused on a new target.

## Alternative reading
Problem-first is a distant second reading if one treats "achieving lower crosstalk in MCFs" as an old, well-established engineering target that the paper merely optimizes further with known techniques, but the abstract's explicit claim of a new derivation method (not just an incremental parameter tweak) makes means-first the better fit.

## Notes for the codebook
A record-setting engineering result explicitly attributed to a newly derived design/analysis model is a clean means-first case; the "instrument" vs "formalisation" move labels overlap heavily here (a new analytical tool that is also a new equation), and the codebook could clarify which one to prefer when a paper's central move is a new predictive/design formalism used once to build a device.
