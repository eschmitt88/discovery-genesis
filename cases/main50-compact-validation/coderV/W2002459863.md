## Reasoning
The self-citation density (17 of 71) directly links this paper to the authors' own 2007 smaller-scale pan-genome study and their 2008 pan-genome modeling framework, and the full text explicitly claims this as the largest sample to date — the clean signature of `gap-filling:bigger-n` rather than a new method or instrument. All analytical tools (Mauve, RAxML, MLST, the supragenome/power-law models) are established and largely the authors' own.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a weak alternative if one credits the inclusion of related Mitis-group species (not just more S. pneumoniae strains) as a qualitatively new capability, but the methodology applied to those additional genomes is identical to that used on S. pneumoniae, so the scale-up (more strains, more species, same pipeline) reads as bigger-n rather than a new capability.

## Notes for the codebook
This is a clean `gap-filling:bigger-n` case at genome scale (more strains/species, same pipeline, explicitly self-described as the largest sample to date) and could serve as a good large-consortium worked example alongside the codebook's clinical-trial/cohort examples for this label.
