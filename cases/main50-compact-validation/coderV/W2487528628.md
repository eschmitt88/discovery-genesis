## Reasoning
The paper's own framing is explicitly comparative-methodological: prior studies "vary widely in cell type, number of samples, and even analysis platform," and a specific recent claim (chromatin-domain dysregulation) is directly re-examined and rejected using this paper's larger, matched dataset — pointing to problem-first (an old, specific, contested question closed with existing tools deployed more rigorously and at greater scale) rather than a new capability. All three self-citations are to the authors' own pipeline-validation work, not prior trisomy-21 biology, consistent with reusing an established but not trisomy-21-specific toolkit.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is a plausible alternative if the deliberate pairing of RNA-seq transcriptomics with an orthogonal kinome-wide shRNA screen (rather than either alone) is read as a qualitatively new combined-capability design that made the consistent signal detectable for the first time, rather than merely a bigger, better-controlled version of prior single-method studies.
