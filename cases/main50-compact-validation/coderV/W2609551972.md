## Reasoning
The overwhelming self-citation density (19 of 36), including the tool's own three prior major versions and nearly every individual algorithm being newly integrated, marks this as a platform-consolidation update rather than a new scientific finding — problem-first in the sense that the "problem" (keeping a community tool current) is closed by assembling already-existing (mostly the authors' own) component algorithms rather than inventing a new one. The paper explicitly self-labels as a versioned software release.

## Alternative reading
Means-first could apply to individual sub-features (e.g. the new trans-AT PKS alignment method is described as genuinely new, "manuscript in preparation"), but the paper as a whole is dominated by integration of pre-existing components rather than one unifying new capability, so consolidation/problem-first better describes the paper's overall genesis.

## Notes for the codebook
Versioned software-tool papers are a distinct genre the genesis_model decision order does not cleanly address (there is no single "finding" to trace through the cascade); `is_primary: partial — tool paper` plus `consolidation` as the move is a reasonable pairing, but the codebook could benefit from an explicit worked example for major-version tool-update papers as a class, since they recur across bioinformatics.
