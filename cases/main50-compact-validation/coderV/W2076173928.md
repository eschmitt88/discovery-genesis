## Reasoning
The dense run of SELF-marked citations (2008, 2011, 2013, 2014) on glycated albumin utility and its ratio to HbA1c signals serial reuse of the authors' own established measurement platform, which the v1.1 tell assigns to problem-first rather than means-first. The specific open question — how to get an accurate glycaemic indicator in non-dialysis ESRD — was already named in their own 2011 paper, and this paper answers it with a regression correction rather than a new instrument.

## Alternative reading
Means-first is plausible if the adjustment formula itself counts as a new capability, but it is a straightforward regression correction rather than a new assay or instrument, so problem-first fits the v1.1 decision order better.

## Notes for the codebook
Small incremental clinical-chemistry refinements like this (an adjustment formula, not a new assay) sit awkwardly between `new-assay-or-method` and `existing-resource-repurposed` in the enabler vocabulary; the codebook could use a one-line rule for "same assay, new correction term."
