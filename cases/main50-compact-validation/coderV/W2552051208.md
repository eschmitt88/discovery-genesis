## Reasoning
The dense self-citation cluster shows this paper as a direct extension of the authors' own well-established directed-forgetting paradigm (already used to study age effects in 2013), now applied to new population subgroups defined by sex and hormonal status — the v1.1 next-target pattern (established method, new population) rather than a new experimental capability.

## Alternative reading
Idea-first is a plausible alternative if one credits the sex-hormone hypothesis (that estradiol/progesterone selectively affect cue-specific processing) as a stated proposition driving the design, but the abstract's structure (multiple population subgroups, multiple hormone correlations reported post hoc by condition) reads more as systematic exploration within an established paradigm than a single pre-stated hypothesis test.
