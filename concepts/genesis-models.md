# Genesis models

## Definition

The four candidate shapes for how a paper's contribution arose from its
prior art: **idea-first** (proposition → work), **means-first** (new
instrument / data / compute used exploratorily → result → idea recognised
afterwards), **problem-first** (old open problem meets newly available
ingredient; the move was nearly forced), **accretion** (no single locatable
idea; impact from consolidation, timing, execution, or being the reference
everyone needed).

## Why it matters here

The naive chain *prior art → novel idea → impactful paper* assumes
idea-first. H0 says it is the minority case. Coding every [[genesis-card]]
with a model — and recording the evidence — is how the project avoids
forcing the intermediate step. The skill that comes out looks very
different depending on the answer: an idea generator (idea-first), a
"what just became possible" scanner (means-first / problem-first), or a
problem-selection and timing guide (accretion).

## Connections

- The authors' own narrative is recorded separately
  ([[hindsight-narrative-bias]]); introductions over-report idea-first.
- Problem-first is the [[adjacent-possible]] made concrete; Merton's
  multiples are its fingerprint.
- `[[dunbar1997scientists]]`'s in-vivo lab studies and Hacking's
  "experiments have a life of their own" are the means-first evidence
  base: in four molecular-biology labs, unexpected findings were common
  (18 of 70 conditions) and drew *more* group attention than expected
  ones (176 vs. 23 interactions), reasoned about via local within-domain
  analogy rather than a stated prior idea.
- `[[simonton2010bvsr]]`'s blind-variation/selective-retention theory is
  the strongest theoretical (not just observational) reason idea-first
  self-reports may be sighted reconstructions of a genuinely blinder
  search — its "sightedness" parameter is a candidate confidence
  dimension for the card's `genesis_model` field.
- `[[merton1961singletons]]`'s multiples are problem-first's fingerprint
  in the record: near-simultaneous independent discovery.
- `[[sourati2023accelerating]]` operationalises the idea-first/problem-
  first boundary computationally: discoveries predictable from the
  field's current distribution of human attention are (by this project's
  terms) problem-first/in-the-adjacent-possible; its "alien hypotheses"
  — plausible but unlikely to be imagined by anyone soon — are the
  closest existing operational proxy for idea-first.
