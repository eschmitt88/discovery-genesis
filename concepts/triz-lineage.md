# TRIZ lineage

## Definition

Altshuller's programme: read a very large number of patents, keep the
inventive ones, and extract the recurring principles (40 inventive
principles, the contradiction matrix, laws of technical-system
evolution). The closest precedent for "read impactful papers, extract the
moves, ship a procedure".

## Why it matters here

It shows the deliverable is possible and shows its failure modes: the
exemplars were hand-picked (no random sampling, no controls), the
principles are generic enough to fit anything after the fact, and the
evidence that TRIZ users out-invent non-users is thin. This project's
sampling, twins and retrodiction test are each a response to one of those.

## Connections

- Polya's *How to Solve It* and Hamming's *You and Your Research* are the
