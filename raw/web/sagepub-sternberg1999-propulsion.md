## The model (cross-checked public summary)

Sternberg proposes eight types of creative contribution, defined by how a
contribution relates to the field's current paradigm/accepted way of
seeing a domain, sorted into three families:

- **Paradigm-accepting**: *replication* (shows the field is in the right
  place), *redefinition* (reframes where the field already is),
  *forward incrementation* (moves the field one step further in its
  current direction — the modal, least risky contribution),
  *advance forward incrementation* (moves further than the field is ready
  for).
- **Paradigm-rejecting**: *redirection* (moves the field in a new
  direction from where it is), *reconstruction/redirection back to a
  point* (moves back to where the field was, then in a new direction),
  *reinitiation* (starts the field over from a new point).
- **Paradigm-integrating**: *integration* (synthesizes two previously
  distinct approaches into one).

The taxonomy is explicitly a rival/complement to Kuhn's normal-science /
paradigm-shift dichotomy — most contributions (forward incrementations)
accept the paradigm; the rarer redirection/reinitiation types reject it.
