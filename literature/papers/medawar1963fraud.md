# Revisiting "Is the scientific paper a fraud?"

## TL;DR

A 2014 EMBO Reports revisit of Medawar's 1963 argument that the IMRaD
research paper narrates a tidy, inductive genesis (hypothesis → predicted
consequence → confirming experiment) that misrepresents how research
actually happens — false starts, accidental findings, ideas that arrive
after data collection. Howitt & Wilson argue undergraduate teaching still
trains students on this fraudulent tidy form, both as writers and as
readers who come to expect (and misremember) their own research the same
way.

## Claims

- The standard published paper's narrative structure (stated hypothesis,
  deduced test, confirming result) is a stylized reconstruction, not a
  report of the actual reasoning sequence that produced the result.
- This is not just a rhetorical quirk: because students are taught to
  write (and read) papers in this form, it actively shapes how the next
  generation of scientists frames — and later *remembers* — their own
  discovery process, reinforcing hindsight narrative bias at the
  training stage rather than only at the publication stage.
- The distortion is worst for genuinely exploratory or accidental
  findings, which get retrofitted into an idea-first narrative that did
  not occur.

## Methods

- Argumentative/pedagogical essay (not an empirical study): revisits
  Medawar's original claim in light of how research methods and
  scientific-writing courses are taught, drawing on textbook and
  research-article examples.
